stance and participation
When Contextual Abstention is enabled, the stance policy decides when the character responds, defers, asks for clarification, or stays silent. If the kernel provides geometric state, stance thresholds are nudged by ±15% based on coherence, stability, and social resonance — but behavior remains deterministic without it. Three levels: stance off (always respond), stance on without geometry (rule-based), stance on with geometry (kernel-modulated rules).
Contextual Abstention
Allows the character to decline, defer, remain silent, or wait for a better context before responding. Enables alive-feeling behavior where the character chooses when and how to participate rather than always answering directly.
