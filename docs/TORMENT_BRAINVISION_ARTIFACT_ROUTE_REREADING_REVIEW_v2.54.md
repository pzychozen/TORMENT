## 4. Review Point 2 — Did v2.53 avoid recomputation, new measurements, new evidence fields, and raw-data reinterpretation?

```text

Brainvision numeric value appears anywhere in v2.53.

PARTIAL REFUTATION -- AND THIS IS THE REVIEW'S FIRST REAL CATCH:

  v2.53's L4 asserts that the word "closure" carries at least two unrelated senses in the record: a TECHNICAL PROPERTY
  studied in the chroma line, and BRANCH TERMINATION. The observation about branch termination is solid -- it is what
```

## 9. Review Point 7 — Wording risk: where could artifact VULNERABILITY be mistaken for artifact CONCLUSION?

```text
THREE RESIDUAL WORDING RISKS. This is the review's second real catch, and the more serious one.

W1 -- SECTION 4's PREAMBLE. v2.53 wrote that its own content is "the strongest evidence yet assembled in the record's own
      terms" that the loop has started producing confidence instead of falsification pressure. Read strictly, that is a
      statement ABOUT OUR PROCESS and it is defensible. Read at speed -- and documents are read at speed -- it lands as
      A FINDING THAT THE LOOP IS PRODUCING PROCEDURAL CONFIDENCE. That is the negative rescued, in the very document
      that forbids rescuing it.
      DISPOSITION: HELD LOOSELY. The correct statement is narrower: THE SCAN ASSEMBLED A PATTERN THAT WOULD BE
      CONSISTENT WITH THE LOOP PRODUCING PROCEDURAL CONFIDENCE, AND CANNOT DISTINGUISH THAT FROM A LOOP APPLYING
      LEGITIMATE PRESSURE SLOWLY. It said as much elsewhere ("this document cannot tell which from the inside"). The two
      statements are in tension, and the narrower one governs.

W2 -- "A WELL-GUARDED EMPTY ROOM IS STILL EMPTY" (L5). The metaphor's PAYLOAD is correct and important: restraint
      prevents false claims and produces no true ones; the boundary apparatus is a property of our caution, not of our
      evidence. But the SENTENCE asserts that the room is empty. It is a forbidden negative conclusion in miniature,
      smuggled by a figure of speech.
      DISPOSITION: the payload is KEPT; the assertion is WITHDRAWN. Correct form: GUARDS ARE NOT FURNITURE. Whether the
      room contains anything is exactly what the record does not know.

W3 -- THE ACCUMULATED RHETORICAL FORCE. v2.53 is, taken as a whole, more PERSUASIVE than its claim-status permits. Seven
      lenses, seven patterns, each individually hedged, produce in aggregate the strong impression that the case is
      made. Nothing in it says the case is made. Everything in it TILTS that way -- and a reader who takes away "the
      record is mostly procedural confidence" will not be able to point to the sentence that told them so, because there
      isn't one. That is the most dangerous kind of overreach, because it cannot be quoted and therefore cannot be
      corrected line by line.
      DISPOSITION: RECORDED, NOT REPAIRED. A synthesis, if one is written, must state the non-conclusion FIRST and at
      full strength -- not as a closing caveat after the case has been made.

CONSEQUENTLY: artifact_ruled_out = False. artifact explanation proven / established: NOT CLAIMED and FORBIDDEN.
ARTIFACT-ROUTE VULNERABILITY REMAINS SUSPICION PRESSURE ONLY -- and the negative reading, exactly as with the proxy
branch before it, REMAINS LEGITIMATE BUT UNCONCLUDED.
```
