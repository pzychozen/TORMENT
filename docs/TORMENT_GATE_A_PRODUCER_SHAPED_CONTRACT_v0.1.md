**Layer 1 requirement-level producer-shaped contract. Docs-only. NON-AUTHORIZING.**

candidate-shaped output would have to be and not be — **not an API contract and not
a runtime contract.** It defines requirements only; it builds nothing.

Held true throughout:

- **No runtime producer exists** (and none is authorized here).
- **No candidate boundary, no candidate-holding mechanism, and no admission
  mechanism is selected** here.
- **No seam is selected.**
- **No wall mechanics are opened.**
- **No Gate D / private cognition is opened.**
- **No code, no tests, no git, no §0 pointer, no other docs.**

This artifact may shape context. It must not seize authority.

## 3. Doctrine filter

> Memory may shape context. Memory may not seize authority.
> Audit observes authority. Audit must not become authority.

A requirement that could only be met by violating this filter is out of scope by

## 4. Future candidate-shaped output — requirement-level meaning

The controlled wording of this contract:

> A future producer, if separately authorized later, would be constrained to
> produce only candidate-shaped outputs.
> Those outputs would remain inside a future separately defined candidate boundary.
> This contract does not define, build, store, route, serialize, expose, admit, or
> promote those outputs.

At requirement level, a **candidate-shaped output** would be a reflection-derived
item that is **explicitly staged** for possible later consideration — never an item
that counts as anything merely by existing. It would be inspectable at the level of
its existence, lineage, and status; contestable; resettable; and recoverable. A
reflection synthesis would become candidate-shaped **only when explicitly staged**,
not before. This contract states that requirement-level meaning and nothing more.

## 5. What a candidate-shaped output must never be

A candidate-shaped output must never be: **canonical**; **authority-bearing**;
**prompt-visible**; **writer-reaching**; **reentrant** into cognition; or **pinned**. It must never be **silently or
automatically admitted into ordinary memory, or promoted, by virtue of being
produced, staged, or inspected** — a future governed admission remains a separate,
separately-authorized decision. Its existence confers no weight, no eligibility,
and no rights.

## 6. What a candidate-shaped output must never touch

It must never reach, influence, or feed any of:

- the ordinary ingest root and its fan-out;
- the ordinary graph lanes;
- motif / drift / gravity / mood / role / deep / SRG;
- reinforcement (count / strength / last-reinforced);
- ordinary retrieval;
- archive→core or any promotion;
- canon / identity-tier / seed / long-half-life material;
- prompt-visible or caller-visible projection;
- any writer.

## 7. Non-authority rules

- **creation ≠ admission ≠ promotion** — producing a candidate-shaped output
  grants it nothing; any later movement is a separate, separately-authorized
  decision (referenced in §8, undefined here).
- **inspection ≠ authority ≠ projection** — observing a candidate-shaped output
  is operator/governance-auditable visibility only; it does not make it
  authoritative and does not make it prompt/caller/retrieval/cognition-visible
  (A-I1 / A-C3).
- **containment by construction, not tag filtering** — the obligation that these
  outputs do not reach §6 must hold structurally, never by downstream readers
  honoring an exclusion tag (A-C2).
- **soft / resettable / contestable / recoverable** — a candidate-shaped output
  is soft state: it may be inspected, contested, reset, and recovered, and is
  never canonical or authority-bearing.

## 8. Relation to the later wall (requirement-reference level only)

This Layer-1 contract **references** but does **not** define the rest of the stack:

- the future candidate boundary and any future governed admission or governed
  promotion are **later, separately authorized** decisions (Layer 3 and beyond);
  this contract names them only as the obligations a candidate-shaped output is
  subject to, and **does not absorb Layer 3** (no boundary or admission
  requirements are stated here);
- **where the wall would live (seam selection) is Layer 2 and is not chosen here**;
- **runtime mechanics are Layer 4** and **Gate D / private cognition is Layer 5**,
  both separately authorized and out of scope.

The producer-shaped obligations above are written so that a future wall *could*
hold them — without this contract opening the wall, choosing a seam, or defining
how containment, admission, or promotion would work.
