## 3. Q5 boundary: write-side relation, not writer mechanism

Each row below states **what the future crossing+carrier must expose / support** so that a later Gate B
durable writer-authority resolution can attach, the Gate B durable writer-resolution obligation it serves,
and the reading that is **forbidden** (to keep it relation-level, not a mechanism). Every obligation is
**writer-mechanism-agnostic**: it says *what must be exposed / supported*, never *how* a writer resolves,
promotes, classifies, authorizes, persists, or crosses. "The relation must let a resolution attach only to a
governed authority-bearing write" is an obligation on the exposed relation; it is **not** a writer, writer
policy, classifier, `mtype` schema, promotion path, governance vehicle, or durable-resolution store
authorized here.
