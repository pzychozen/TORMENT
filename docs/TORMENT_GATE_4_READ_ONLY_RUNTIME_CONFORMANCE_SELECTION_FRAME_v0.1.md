## 3. Why Gate 4 precedes Gate 1

`[FACT]` Gate 1's chamber / private-continuity design depends on **live-path non-reachability**. The
Private-Cognition / Unified-Reflection Blueprint requires, for each mode and each interior artifact
class, a structural by-construction demonstration of non-reachability against the ordinary fan-out — and
states that the Regime-A obligation must hold against the **live retrieval / prompt-assembly path**, not
only the durable graph, naming it the hardest proof obligation and the most likely leak point. The
Candidate-Containment / Writer-Authority Contract makes the same containment invariant load-bearing and
requires it to hold **structurally, not by tag-honoring**, citing the `ws_section_2a_v1` precedent that
auto-emitted identity pressure can occur once material enters ordinary fan-out.

`[FACT]` Existing old mechanisms already shape identity pressure **indirectly** today — this is
already-characterized, point-in-time fact from the influence traces and the writer-authority hazard
inventory, carried here without remedy and without a safety verdict. Because that floor is real and
currently **unframed for conformance**, a chamber boundary designed on top of it would be designed
against paths whose conformance posture has not yet been recorded.

`[FACT]` Therefore the ordering: the live identity-pressure floor should be **framed read-only against
existing boundaries first** (Gate 4 selection level), before Gate 1 chamber/private-continuity design.
This is a sequencing statement about *framing*, not a remedy, not an enforcement step, and not a claim
that the floor is unsafe.

`[DISTINCTION]` `old identity-pressure paths ≠ chamber / private-cognition design.` Framing the old
paths' conformance posture is observing the floor; it is **not** designing, opening, or pre-empting the
chamber interior (Document B), which remains deferred.
