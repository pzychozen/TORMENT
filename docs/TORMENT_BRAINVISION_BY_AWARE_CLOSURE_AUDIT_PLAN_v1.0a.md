## 5. BY-aware audit panels

Each panel is a **reporting requirement only** — not a new closure metric, not a pass/fail gate; it does not
redefine `TOL` and does not move claim locks:

```text
A. Signed-offset representation panel
   Report by_centroid / by_spread / by_std signed offsets, sign consistency, and directionality.
B. Opponent-axis dominance panel
   Compare BY magnitude against the RG and directional features.
C. Binding-stat representation panel
   Report how often BY stats bind the residual match, especially by_std.
D. Aggregation-warning panel
   Report cases where the residual / TOL match COEXISTS with a systematic BY ordering.
E. Coupling / leakage separation panel
   Distinguish BY_axis_asymmetry from centroid/spread coupling and amplitude / channel-energy leakage.
F. Region / family caveat panel
   Report whether BY visibility varies by target region or candidate family, preserving the single-matching-
   family (segment_paired_canceller) caveat.
G. Non-authorizing visibility panel
   Explicitly report that BY visibility is DIAGNOSTIC ONLY and does NOT authorize descriptor validity, pass/fail,
   runtime, memory, integration, or vision claims (implements requirement G as an explicit panel).
```
