## 0. Purpose

This document does not select a carrier or a composition. It does not answer whether a composition is needed. It frames **whether the composition question may be studied at all** — and whether that question can be preserved as an unresolved structural question without collapsing into architecture.

## 1. Inherited Boundary

This frame inherits, by reference and without re-litigation: `docs/PROJECT_ORIENTATION_MAP.md` §0 (living authority; FORMAL HOLD; Mode 0); the carrier/root operator decision record (`ab5a3a2` — Hilmir Form C: the composition question advances as a question only); the carrier/root selection frame (`b8b87ba`, Codex-reviewed); the carrier/substrate root survey, closed and hardened (`9fac9c6`/`74bb0da`); Document A Q1–Q7 requirement frames; the operator-only admission floor (`62400c9`, unweakened); the Gate A wall enforcement-path proposal (`b84191b`, non-selecting); the Gate B / H3 inventory constraints (H1–H6, H3 `force=True` parked); and the pre-carrier representation constraints (P-1…P-9 / A-1…A-8). Every boundary in those artifacts binds this frame; none is summarized here in a way that could substitute for its source.

## 2. The Question Being Asked

> **Can TORMENT study whether a carrier composition is needed at all?**

**Containment relation (binding condition 1):** this frame is the **admissibility step** of the question made eligible by `ab5a3a2`; it does **not** replace that question, and **no outcome of this frame answers whether a composition is needed.**

This is not: composition selection; carrier selection; architecture; implementation; or future scheduling. A "yes" to admissibility would mean only that the question may exist as legitimate, unresolved study terrain; a "no" would mean only that it may not — yet, or in this form.

## 3. The Question Not Being Asked

This frame does not ask, and may not be read as asking:

- which composition;
- which family;
- which pairing;
- whether any specific combination of surveyed families should exist together;
- whether any stack, layer, hybrid, bridge, relay, hierarchy, or sequence should exist.

It also decides neither direction of the substantive question: it does **not** decide that composition is needed, and it does **not** decide that composition is not needed. It does not decide that a single carrier family is sufficient, nor that multiple are required. It expresses no preference among F1–F5 or any future family.

**Soft-selection vocabulary is not adopted by this document.** Terms such as "likely," "best," "natural," "obvious," "primary," "foundation," "core stack," "minimal viable carrier," "recommended composition," and "candidate architecture" appear here only inside this prohibition and carry no selecting force anywhere in this frame or in any artifact citing it.

Composition may be discussed only while ALL of the following hold:

1. FORMAL HOLD remains active.
2. Mode 0 remains active.
3. No carrier family is selected.
4. No composition is selected, sketched, preferred, named, ranked, or implied.
5. F1–F5 remain non-exhaustive.
6. Eligibility remains separate from authorization.

**Bar summary (precondition, not headline):** composition may be named only as an **unresolved structural question**. It may not be treated as a solution, direction, dependency, or future commitment. The "preserve the question as unresolved" posture is the precondition for admissibility — it is not, and may not become, the answer to whether a composition is needed.

## 5. Anti-Implicit-Composition Guard

No example set, family list, or prior survey grouping may be read as a proposed composition. In particular, references to F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, or any future Fn do **not** imply pairwise, triple, layered, sequential, fallback, bridge, or hybrid structure. The hardened survey's complementary-coverage observation remains a fact about matrix cells only.

**Forbidden-shape ban, by category only (binding condition 3).** The following *categories* of expression are forbidden in this frame and in any artifact citing it as authority, with **zero concrete instances printed anywhere in this frame, including inside this ban**:

- functional-role assignments to families;
- layer/tier/temperature metaphors;
- technology-name compositions;
- ordering/sequence/fallback structures;
- bridge/hybrid/relay shapes.

Abstract neutral placeholders ("family A," "family B," "family N," "one or more unresolved carrier families," "any hypothetical composition") are permitted — and are themselves guarded: a placeholder may not accrete descriptive content, role content, or ordering content that would turn it into an example.

## 6. Non-Exhaustiveness Guard

F1–F5 are inherited from the prior survey as **non-exhaustive reference families**. They do not define the available search space, the allowed solution set, or the boundary of future carrier thought. Omission is not rejection. They are deliberately **not re-listed or re-described here**: re-listing risks turning this frame back into a menu, and this frame is not a menu.

A future family may be admitted to discussion if it satisfies the same non-authorization constraints as F1–F5. Novelty does not make it privileged; absence from the prior list does not make it excluded.

## 7. Equal-Treatment Rule for Newly Named Families

Any newly named family must enter at the same level as F1–F5: **non-selected, non-preferred, non-authorized, and non-exhaustive.** It may not be used to bypass the carrier survey, the operator-only admission floor, Gate A/B/D, H3 constraints, or any later authorization boundary. A newly named family receives no advantage for being new; a previously named family receives no advantage for being already documented. Any family, old or new, remains only an object of study. Naming a family does not make it eligible; eligibility does not authorize selection; selection, if ever allowed, would require a separate gate.

## 8. Gating After Any Answer

Even if this frame's admissibility question is eventually answered "yes," ALL of the following remain separately gated, each requiring explicit Hilmir authorization plus a Codex challenge: carrier family selection; composition selection; substrate/database choice; schema definition; field definition; ID scheme; API/seam design; admission mechanics; promotion mechanics; crossing mechanics; Gate A; Gate B / H3; Gate D; the operator-only admission floor (which may be strengthened, never weakened); the pre-carrier representation constraints; memory-to-prompt wiring; runtime/provider paths; Dream/private cognition; autonomy; MCP/action; Brainvision; audio/environment translation; and implementation of any kind.

**Central firewall:** a positive answer to the composition-question admissibility problem would **not authorize composition**. It would only authorize further **below-implementation study** of whether composition can be asked without collapsing into architecture.

## 9. Failure Modes

The dangerous collapses this frame exists to prevent, named so they can be caught:

- the question becomes architecture;
- examples become composition;
- the family list becomes a menu;
- eligibility becomes authorization;
- docs-only becomes a roadmap;
- HOLD becomes "pending implementation";
- "composition" becomes database/substrate by implication;
- a placeholder accretes content until it is an example;
- an admissibility "yes" is read as a substantive "needed."

Any artifact exhibiting one of these collapses may not cite this frame as authority.
