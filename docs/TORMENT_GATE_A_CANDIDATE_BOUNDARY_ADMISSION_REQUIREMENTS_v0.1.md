## 4. The three requirement-level concepts (kept distinct)

### 4.1 Candidate boundary — a containment property
The candidate boundary is a **requirement-level containment property**: the
requirement that candidate-shaped outputs remain non-reachable to the Document A
A-C1 sites and remain inspectable without re-entry (A-C3) for their whole
existence. It is a **property the producer's outputs must satisfy — not a place, an
object, a store, or a container,** and not a data shape.

### 4.2 Governed admission — a crossing condition
Governed admission is a **requirement-level crossing condition**: the only governed
condition under which a candidate-shaped output may be considered for ordinary
memory (A-O3 — admission is the sole exit), and only at **no higher than a released / low-authority posture**
(A-D1). The condition is that such a crossing be **explicit, recorded, and
contestable**. It is stated at requirement level **only — not a procedure, a
workflow, an actor sequence, a trigger, an API, or an implementation.** "Recorded"
here is a requirement only: **no format, log, event, store, database, or
persistence is selected.**

### 4.3 Promotion crossing — a separate authority increase
A promotion crossing is a **separate, separately-authorized requirement-level
authority increase**: any move beyond the released / low-authority posture toward
identity-shaping or canon is a **distinct** governed promotion crossing, **never
folded into admission** (A-D2). Any revocation or reclassification likewise
requires **its own** separate crossing. This is an **authority-posture requirement,
not a mechanism.**

## 5. Anti-collapse lines

Collapsing any of these is drift:

- **boundary ≠ store / container** — the boundary is a containment property, not a
  thing that holds candidates.
- **admission ≠ procedure / implementation** — admission is a condition, not a
  worked-out sequence.
- **released / low-authority ≠ schema / enum / field** — it is an authority
  posture, not a representation.
- **recorded ≠ ledger / store selected** — "recorded" is a requirement; no log,
  event, or holding place is chosen.
- **reversal / reclassification / revocation ≠ reversal mechanics** — each is a
  requirement that a separate governed crossing exist, not a built reversal.
- **inspection ≠ inspection-surface design** — inspectability (A-I1/A-C3) is a
  requirement, not a designed observation surface.
- **seam comparison remains Layer 2** — where a wall could live is not decided
  here.
- **runtime remains Layer 4** — separately authorized; not entered here.
- **Gate D remains Layer 5** — separately authorized; not entered here.

## 6. Preserved invariants

- **creation ≠ admission ≠ promotion.**
- **existence, staging, and inspection do not admit or promote** a candidate-shaped
  output (A-I1/A-C3, A-D1/A-D2).
- **admission is a ceiling, not a guarantee** — it lands at no higher than released
  / low-authority and does not guarantee ordinary-memory entry.
- **no automatic, retrieval-driven, or reinforcement-driven admission or
  promotion** — both are governed decisions, never side effects of being produced,
  retrieved, reinforced, staged, or inspected.
- **"recorded" is requirement-level only** — no format, log, event, store,
  database, endpoint, or persistence is selected.
- **released / low-authority is an authority posture only** — no representation,
  Cluster-2 vehicle, storage, or Seed-Gov mechanic is selected.
