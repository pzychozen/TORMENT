## §4 Admissibility bar (what a candidate must carry to appear on the menu)

Every menu entry, surveyed or newly named, must carry all six statements. A missing statement disqualifies the entry from the menu; it does not reject the family (omission is not rejection).

1. **Q1 coverage statement** — position against all ten Q1 properties; any CANNOT on a property the candidate claims to serve must be paired with an explicitly **named compensating obligation**, never silently absorbed.
2. **Envelope conformance statement** — P-1…P-9 preservation and A-1…A-8 exposure, every tension named, with a mitigation **obligation** (not a design) attached.
3. **Forbidden-inference block** — what may not be inferred from the candidate: no source-id field/versioning token/hash/matcher implied; presence is inert; nothing satisfies the operator-only floor; no tag-honoring containment.
4. **F5 delta** — an argument from the frozen frames only for why the null baseline does not already provide what the candidate claims. No new experiments, data, or reruns.
5. **Relation statements** — Q4 (P4 exposure), Q5 (Gate B attachability incl. H3 non-inheritance), Q6 (B-O9 staging-is-not-admission) positions.
6. **Process gate** — this frame must pass a Codex challenge before any decision record may cite it.

## §6 Decision forms available to Hilmir (exactly three; none pre-selected)

- **Form A — advance one family.** One surveyed-or-newly-named family (with its §4 statements complete) becomes **eligible for a separately authorized design frame**. Required follow-on gates: Hilmir authorization of that design frame + Codex challenge of it; everything in §8 untouched.
- **Form B — advance the composition question as a question.** No family advances; instead a future, separately authorized frame may study whether a composition is needed at all. This form does not name, sketch, or prefer any composition. Required follow-on gates: same as Form A, for the composition-question frame.
- **Form C — none advances; HOLD.** A complete answer, co-equal with A and B. Declining to choose is a valid instance of Form C.

No form is recommended by this frame. No form auto-executes; each requires the standalone decision record of §10.

## §7 What selection does NOT confer

A recorded selection (any form) confers **eligibility for one separately authorized design frame, and nothing else**. It does not authorize design, mechanics, representation, code, tests, storage, migration, seam choice, wall enforcement, Gate B resolution, admission/promotion/crossing behavior, or any downstream lane. Eligibility ≠ authorization (§K). Selection creates no schedule, no momentum, and no obligation; a selected candidate can sit indefinitely without its design frame ever being authorized.
