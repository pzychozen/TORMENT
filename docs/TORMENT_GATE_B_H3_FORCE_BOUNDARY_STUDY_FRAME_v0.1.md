## §4 Operator-floor force vs. transmissible system authority

**Operator-floor force** is a governed human act: singular, crossing-scoped, fresh (non-replayable), evidenced, contestable, revocable-before-effect, satisfiable by nothing less than the operator — the Q3 properties applied to an override. It is an *event* with a witness record.

**Transmissible system authority** is what force becomes the moment its *effect* carries a status that any downstream consumer reads as elevated legitimacy — when "this was forced in" functions anywhere as "this outranks."

**The boundary, stated once:** *force may change what exists; it may never change what what-exists is worth.* An act that ends at the crossing is floor. An act that echoes as rank is leash. H3's present hazard is exactly that `force=True` bypasses governance **and** leaves no structure preventing its result from being consumed as ordinarily legitimate — the bypass launders itself. This frame names that hazard; it does not fix it.

## §5 Preserved invariants — what any future force/override path must satisfy

In addition to all Q3 floor obligations, four requirement-level invariants:

1. **Attributable-without-privileging.** A forced effect must be permanently marked as forced (Q2 occurrence evidence) while the mark confers zero rank in any consumer.
2. **No self-laundering.** No later process — reinforcement, promotion, retrieval, annotation, reappend, migration — may wash the mark off or convert it into ordinary legitimacy.
3. **Audit-visible, cognition-inert.** Audit and operator surfaces see force plainly and completely; cognition, retrieval, routing, and generation cannot condition on it.
4. **Downward contestability.** A forced effect remains reversible and contestable by the same floor that created it, forever; contest routes down, never up into new authority.

## §7 Relation to M3 — live surfaces remain closed

One requirement, pinned now, before any gate chain is ever proposed: **a future live surface may never issue, invoke, request, or perceive force.** The model is not a force consumer (force marks are not model-visible as rank), not a force issuer (no output may trigger or request an override), and not a force beneficiary (its generated content receives no privileged write path). This binds the recorded M3 *direction* at requirement level and touches no wire, opens no gate, and brings nothing nearer. If a live surface is someday separately gated into existence, it arrives into a system where force is something done *at* the floor by its keeper, witnessed — never something flowing through the resident.

## §8 Relation to MCP/action/movement — those lanes remain closed

Force/override semantics are **memory-side only**. An override of what is remembered is never an override of what is done: no force path may reach MCP (which stays read/advisory/reactive), any action surface, or render-body movement (scene-disjoint sink; movement-as-action excluded per the render-body non-coupling firewall). The converse binds equally: **no action-side urgency — "the body needs it," "the tool requires it," "the scene demands it" — may ever justify a memory-side force act.** Pressure does not flow backward across the boundary. Nothing in this section opens, describes, or schedules any MCP/action/movement capability.

## §9 The retire-vs-constrain fork — framed, unanswered

Whether TORMENT should **retire** force entirely (no override path exists at all) or **constrain** force (an override path exists, bound by §5's invariants) is a genuine operator fork. This frame takes no position. Arguments exist in both directions; none are rehearsed here, because rehearsing them would begin answering. The fork belongs to a **future standalone Hilmir decision record**, written only after separate explicit authorization, and this frame's invariants are drafted to be necessary under *either* answer: if force is retired they describe why nothing may fill its place informally; if force is constrained they describe the constraint floor. **Nothing about this fork is decided, leaned, scheduled, or owed here.**

## §10 Failure modes — named so they can be caught

- **Mark becomes rank** — the provenance mark starts functioning as a legitimacy or priority signal in any consumer.
- **Audit becomes control** — visibility of force acts begins to steer behavior, review, or eligibility ("audited-forced → treated differently by cognition").
- **Study normalizes force** — accumulated boundary study is cited as evidence that force is routine, expected, or pre-approved.
- **One act becomes standing permission** — a recorded force act, or a chain of artifacts citing it, is read as authorization for the next act.
- **Urgency crosses the memory/action boundary** — action-side pressure justifies memory-side force, or memory-side force reaches an action surface.
- **Forced status becomes a cognition input** — any routing, retrieval, salience, confidence, or generation path conditions on force marks.
- **Forced status becomes an authentication substitute** — "it was forced in by the operator" is treated as satisfying identity, provenance, floor, or review requirements it does not satisfy.

Any artifact exhibiting one of these collapses may not cite this frame as authority.
