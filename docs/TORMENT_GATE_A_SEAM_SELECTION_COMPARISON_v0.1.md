# TORMENT Gate A — Seam-Selection Comparison v0.1

## 1. Status / non-selection

**Layer 2. Design-only comparison of possible loci. NON-SELECTING.** This is a
**comparison of possible loci** where a future Gate A containment wall *could*
live. It **selects no seam**, selects no blend, and chooses no mechanics, runtime,
boundary, admission, or promotion. It **does not decide whether the eventual wall
is single-locus or multi-locus.** Each locus below is recorded only as something
that **could bear part of a future wall** and that is **not sufficient alone**.

Anything beyond this comparison **would require later separate authorization**
(Layer 3 boundary/admission requirements, Layer 4 runtime mechanics, Layer 5
Gate D) — none of which is opened here.

This artifact may shape context. It must not seize authority.

## 2. Subordination and the thing-to-be-contained

Subordinate to, and may not contradict: Document A (A-C1/A-C2/A-C3, A-O2/A-O3,
A-I1, A-D1/A-D2), the Gate A wall enforcement frame, and the Layer-1
producer-shaped contract
(`docs/TORMENT_GATE_A_PRODUCER_SHAPED_CONTRACT_v0.1.md`). Where this comparison and

The thing-to-be-contained is the Layer-1 **candidate-shaped output** (a future,
not-yet-existing, explicitly-staged reflection-derived item). No such output is
produced today; this comparison reasons about where a wall *could* hold it if a
producer were ever separately authorized.

## 3. Doctrine filter

> Memory may shape context. Memory may not seize authority.

A locus that could only bear a wall by violating this filter is out of scope by
definition.

## 4. Comparison of possible loci (non-selecting)

Four possible loci. For each: its kind, what it would clarify, why it is **not
sufficient alone**, the forbidden lines it approaches, the future proof
obligations it would later carry, and whether it depends on Layer 3. **No locus is
selected; no combination is selected.**

### Locus 1 — origin-side containment
- **Kind:** origin-side containment locus.
- **What it would clarify:** where a candidate-shaped output is held at its origin
  so it never reaches the §6-class paths of Document A; the natural home of A-C1 /
  A-C2 at the point of production.
- **Not sufficient alone:** there is no producer today to bound (the Layer-1
  producer is design-only); origin-side containment **stays at "origin-side
  containment obligations"** and does not, by itself, establish how anything ever
  legitimately leaves containment.
- **Forbidden lines approached:** boundary/admission territory (routed to Layer 3,
  not entered here).
- **Future proof obligations:** A-C1 (non-reachability held at origin), A-C2
  (structural, not exclusion-tag honoring).
- **Depends on Layer 3:** yes — its meaning needs Layer 3 boundary/admission
  requirements, **which this doc does not define.**

### Locus 2 — `TormentFabric.ingest` ordinary-ingest chokepoint
- **Kind:** downstream chokepoint.
- **What it would clarify:** that the ordinary-ingest fan-out root is a single,
  identified downstream entry a future wall could partly rely on.
- **Terrain fact (not a selection):** `TormentFabric.ingest` is **the most
  source-grounded downstream chokepoint currently identified by inventory**
  (`58d5a49`), **but that is a terrain fact, not a seam selection, and it cannot
  prove containment alone** — it concerns *arrival* at the fan-out root, not
  *origin*, and a chokepoint with nothing upstream to contain is not a wall.
- **Forbidden lines approached:** wall mechanics (if it ever gated anything);
  endpoint / AgentRunner adjacency if mis-scoped.
- **Future proof obligations:** A-C1 (sole-entry, held at the ingest root), A-C2
  (no exclusion-tag honoring; extends `8f8fa7a` to a live producer).
- **Depends on Layer 3:** partial — the terrain is describable now, but what counts
  as an admissible item is Layer 3.

### Locus 3 — writer-authority gate (A-O2 adjacent)
- **Kind:** authority gate — **A-O2 adjacent / later Gate B territory.**
- **What it would clarify:** where cognition/reflection-origin canon / identity-tier
  / seed / long-half-life writes would be refused absent a governed promotion
  (A-O2 / A-O1).
- **Not sufficient alone:** it governs *authority class*, not containment of
  candidate-shaped outputs; it is write-side and sits in **later Gate B territory**.
  **This comparison takes no Gate A action and no fix here.**
- **Forbidden lines approached:** writer territory and the parked writer
  non-conformances (named only as known-parked; not entered, not fixed).
- **Future proof obligations:** A-O2 (no silent canon/identity from cognition),
  A-D2 (admission ≠ promotion).
- **Depends on Layer 3:** yes — admission ≠ promotion separation is Layer 3.

### Locus 4 — inspection surface (A-I1 / A-C3)
- **Kind:** inspection-only surface.
- **What it would clarify:** how candidate-shaped outputs stay operator/governance-
  auditable without becoming projection or re-entry, reusing the proven sealed
  observation posture (`c73add5`, `0dbe7cc`).
- **Not sufficient alone:** **inspection stays observation-only; it is useful for
  A-I1 / A-C3, not for containment** — it observes, it does not contain.
- **Forbidden lines approached:** audit-to-control / prompt exposure if mis-built.
- **Future proof obligations:** A-I1 (inspection ≠ projection), A-C3 (inspectable
  without re-entry).
- **Depends on Layer 3:** lightly — inspecting candidate-shaped outputs presupposes
  Layer 1 more than Layer 3.

## 5. Cross-cutting observations (still non-selecting)

- **Single-locus vs multi-locus is not decided here.** A future wall might rely on
  one locus or several; this comparison records that question and leaves it open.
- **Each locus is not sufficient alone:** Locus 1 is origin-side containment that
  needs Layer 3; Locus 2 is a downstream chokepoint that concerns arrival not
  origin and presupposes a producer; Locus 3 is an authority gate in later Gate B
  territory; Locus 4 is observation-only.
- The Locus 2 terrain fact (most source-grounded downstream chokepoint) is **not a
  seam selection** and does not, on its own, establish containment.
