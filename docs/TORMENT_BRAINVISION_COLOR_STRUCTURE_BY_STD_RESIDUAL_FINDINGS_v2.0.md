## 4. Interpretation

- The `by_std` residual appears when winding / non-winding fixture families have **mismatched baseline
  `by_std` ranges** (e.g. out-and-back non-winders sit at lower `by_std` than the winders).
- When the `by_std` ranges are **matched across winding classes** (winder span vs collinear span), the
  pooled correlation drops **below the 0.30 ceiling** (`-0.193`), i.e. it does not survive matched ranges.
- **PR_collinear** gives **exact** pairwise `by_std` matching (delta `0.0`) while `S`/`PSC` still separates
  the winder from the non-winder.
- Together this supports a **pool-composition artifact**, not a **direct descriptor limitation**, for this
  synthetic diagnostic context.
