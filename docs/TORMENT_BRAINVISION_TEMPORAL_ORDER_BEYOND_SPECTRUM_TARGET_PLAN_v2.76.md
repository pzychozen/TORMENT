## 5. Scientific meaning of order beyond spectrum

Under fixed finite-series, normalization, and boundary conventions, Fourier magnitude / power-spectrum information
constrains the autocorrelation quantities used by an analysis. Practical autocorrelation estimates are not claimed
here to be unconditionally fixed by a loosely defined amplitude spectrum. Two series can share the same or similar
amplitude spectra and still differ in **how their content is arranged in time**. Arrangement or phase
information is therefore **conceptually relevant** to the distinction this target names: organization that survives
when amplitude-spectrum explanations have nothing left to say.

Phase care, stated as hard limits:

```text
NOT stated, here or later:
  phase sensitivity proves temporal order
  phase organization equals perception
  phase differences imply arrow of time
  a phase-sensitive descriptor is scientifically valid
```

"Beyond spectrum" is a **subtraction requirement on explanations**, not a phase claim: whatever a candidate
measurement reports must remain unexplained by the tested roughness, continuity, and amplitude-spectrum alternatives
before the target is even in view. The record's own failures define the bar — SAG fell to roughness/whitening;
recurrence/DET fell to, or remained unresolved under, continuity and spectrum controls. The target remains temporal arrangement,
organization across time, or recurrence structure not fixed by amplitude-spectrum magnitude alone. Surviving
exclusions is necessary but not sufficient; arbitrary descriptor behavior cannot become the target post hoc merely
because known explanations failed. Whether the target exists in measurable form remains **unknown and undecided**.

## 7. Target non-equivalences

```text
order beyond spectrum != arrow of time
  beyond-spectrum organization can be time-symmetric; the record's reversed inputs matched ordered inputs under
  symmetric recurrence. Direction is a separate, harder claim needing direction-sensitive evidence.

order beyond spectrum != directional asymmetry
  same separation: undirected arrangement structure is not true-versus-reversed asymmetry, and neither implies the
  other.

order beyond spectrum != smoothness
  smoothness is accepted roughness/continuity-adjacent explanatory terrain that must be beaten -- not a claim that
  smoothness is mathematically identical to amplitude spectrum in all settings. The record shows smoothness
  producing or contributing to false-positive recurrence behavior, which is why it sits on the EXPLANATION side of
  the subtraction.

order beyond spectrum != continuity
  local adjacency similarity is continuity -- accepted explanatory terrain that must be beaten, not asserted as
  mathematically identical to amplitude spectrum. The recurrence line's failure showed the candidate largely
  tracking, or confounded with, continuity; organization must exceed what continuity already explains.

order beyond spectrum != recurrence score
  any specific score is one candidate instrument; the target is the phenomenon. High recurrence scores arose from
  smooth and spectrum-matched constructions in the record.

order beyond spectrum != phase sensitivity alone
  many statistics change under phase manipulation without that change being meaningful organization; instrument
  sensitivity to a surrogate operation is instrument behavior, not target attainment.

order beyond spectrum != descriptor separation
  separating constructed classes with a descriptor can be construction- or proxy-driven; separation is evidence
  only inside a control frame that removed the cheap explanations.

order beyond spectrum != classifier accuracy
  accuracy on labels can exploit any shortcut in the inputs; classifier and neural routes remain closed.

order beyond spectrum != visual perception
  even the ceiling claim concerns measurement sensitivity on offline descriptor fields, not seeing, meaning, or
  experience.
```

## 12. Conceptual control taxonomy

Conceptual roles and limitations only; **no control is designed here**. One ladder governs every entry:

```text
conceptually required or useful  !=  feasible  !=  honestly constructible  !=  authorized
```

```text
shuffle                       may challenge: adjacency-sensitive or arrangement-sensitive response.
                              cannot establish order when it also changes roughness, continuity, spectrum, or the
                              distribution of transitions -- the record's central shuffle lesson.

reverse                       may challenge: direction-sensitive or arrow-sensitive behavior.
                              cannot challenge: undirected recurrence or symmetric organization.

circular shift                may challenge: absolute start position or phase origin.
                              cannot destroy: local adjacency, cyclic neighborhood structure, undirected recurrence.

spectrum-matched surrogate    may challenge: amplitude-spectrum explanations.
                              cannot by itself establish: nonlinearity, perception, or honest order sensitivity.
                              its construction may itself answer-bake the result, and that hazard travels with it.

smooth-disordered             may challenge: continuity or smoothness false positives.
construction                  "disordered" must not be defined through the candidate measurement or its expected
                              failure.

rough-ordered construction    may challenge: roughness-driven false negatives.
                              "ordered" must not be defined through the candidate measurement or its expected
                              success.

roughness-matched comparison  may challenge: roughness dominance.  feasibility remains unestablished.

continuity-matched comparison may challenge: smoothness or local-adjacency dominance.  feasibility remains
                              unestablished.

phase-altered comparison      may challenge: arrangement dependence under similar amplitude spectra.
                              must not become a phase-specific classifier task, or a construction whose labels
                              define the scientific answer.
```

## 15. Informative-test requirements

An executable script is not an informative scientific test. A future test earns interpretability only through:
predeclared target wording and outcome semantics fixed before data; required positive controls that certify the
instrument before any target reading; the cheap-explanation family (roughness, continuity, amplitude spectrum)
given genuine opportunities to win; construction labels kept separate from interpretations; failure semantics that
can disappoint (an honest negative and an honest UNINFORMATIVE both reachable); and nothing banked from uncertified
runs. These are requirements on any future plan, not steps taken here.
