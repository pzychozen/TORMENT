## 3. Artifact-Route Vulnerability Map

*Qualitative prose only. The seven lenses are SUSPICION LENSES — ways of reading. They are not categories, not bins, not
fields, and nothing is assigned, scored, ranked, or classified by them. Naming a route does not address it.*

### L1 — Fixture construction artifact

The record's fixture line, read only through its own titles and its own accepted conclusions, has a shape worth stating
plainly: **each time construction was suspected, the response was to build another construction.** A fixture bank was
proposed when the descriptor line entangled; a movement-matched fixture was proposed when the bank entangled; a broader
matched-pair diagnostic was proposed when the movement-matched line raised its own questions; a different construction,
and then a matched generative search, were proposed after that. This is not an accusation of bad faith — it is the
obvious engineering instinct, and it produced real work. But as a *route*, it means the record's answer to
"could our construction have made this?" has repeatedly been **a new construction**, and a construction cannot
adjudicate itself.

The sharpest item here is one the record supplies against itself, and this scan neither adds to it nor softens it: the
movement-matched line **attributed its own separation to construction, in its own words**. We did not read that into it.
It is in the accepted summary.

**Vulnerability: LIVE, and partly self-declared.** `artifact_ruled_out` remains False.

### L2 — Symbolic role artifact

The role vocabulary — null roles, adversarial roles, fixture families, protocol guards, family identity — organizes
uncertainty into names that *sound* like an apparatus. Reread coldly, the record shows a long symbolic branch that
produced a vocabulary, audited that vocabulary, hardened its wording, reviewed its invariants, and then **paused as
completed vocabulary**. `role_validated` has been False from the first day of that branch to this one, and it has never
moved.

The suspicion is not that the roles are wrong. It is that **a system of labels can make an unresolved field feel
sorted**, and that a reader — including us — can mistake the tidiness of the labelling for organization in the thing
labelled.

**Vulnerability: LIVE.** `role_validated` and `schema_validated` remain False.

### L3 — Reporting scaffold artifact

This is the lens on which the record is most exposed, and the exposure is visible from the outside of the documents
before one opens any of them.

- The Brainvision line runs to well over a hundred documents across several parallel numbering series.
- Those series each ascend monotonically. **Ascending version numbers read as progress**, and they are not evidence of
  any. A record can climb its own numbering forever without a lock moving, and this one has climbed a long way while
  every lock stayed False.
- The branch rhythm is a **template**: proposal → plan → prereg → implementation review → findings → synthesis →
  decision frame → scan → next proposal. It recurs across the chroma line, the null-first line, the anti-inevitability
  line, and the proxy / confound line. The template is a *format*, and a format applied to nothing produces documents
  shaped exactly like documents applied to something.
- The **lock list** is the strongest formal shape in the record and the weakest scientific object in it. It is a column
  of `False`. It says, in the plainest possible terms, that nothing has been established. It *looks* like an instrument
  panel. A reader skimming any document in this line sees a dense, disciplined, aligned block and feels the presence of
  measurement — and there is none.

**Vulnerability: LIVE and structural.**

### L4 — Document-language artifact

Precision is not evidence. A sentence can be exactly right about nothing, and this record contains a great many
sentences that are exactly right about nothing.

Two specific exposures, both readable from the record's own surface:

- The word **closure** carries at least two unrelated senses here: a *technical property* studied in the chroma line,
  and *branch termination*. A record in which "closure" appears constantly, in both senses, will read as a record in
  which things are being closed.
- The carefully-hedged unresolved state — *remains live*, *remains unresolved*, *reachable but unrecognized* — is
  honest, and it is also **more coherent-sounding than plain speech would be**. Plain speech: *we do not know; we have
  not shown anything; nothing we built has told us whether there is anything there.* That sentence is in the record's
  meaning everywhere and in its wording almost nowhere.

**Vulnerability: LIVE.**

### L5 — Boundary artifact

Quarantine, gating, forbidden registers, claim locks, review prompts, and drift registers are disciplines of
**restraint**. Restraint prevents false claims. It produces no true ones. The record's boundary apparatus is, by now,
elaborate — and its elaborateness is a property of our caution, not of our evidence.

Stated at its bluntest, because the lens exists to permit exactly this: **a well-guarded empty room is still empty**,
and the guards are not furniture. The strength of the boundary work is not a down payment on a result.

**Vulnerability: LIVE.**

### L6 — Closure / synthesis artifact

Reread as a sequence, the record contains **several branches that ended in a pause or in a closure declared to be
non-authorizing** — the symbolic branch paused as completed vocabulary; the null-first role scaffold paused; the
anti-inevitability branch closed as review work that produced no evidence; the proxy / confound rereading branch closed
as suspicion-pressure work that settled nothing.

Each closure was honestly labelled. **The sequence of them still reads as a trajectory.** Four terminations, each
correctly described as non-progress, arranged in ascending order, produce the felt sense of a programme advancing
through stages. It is not advancing through stages. It is terminating branches, and writing the terminations down
carefully, and the care with which they are written is what makes them look like steps.

**Vulnerability: LIVE, and it is the pattern this scan most wants the reviewer to sit with.**

### L7 — Operator / model workflow artifact

`plan → review → accept → commit`. Every stage of that loop produces an artefact that *feels like rigour*:

- a **plan** that binds itself with constraints;
- a **review** — a Codex ACCEPT — which verifies that the document **conforms to its own stated constraints**. That is a
  check of internal consistency and claim hygiene. **It is not a check that anything is true.** An acceptance certifies
  that we said what we said we would say, in the way we said we would say it. It certifies nothing about the world, and
  it cannot;
- a **commit**, which enters the document into the permanent record and produces the strongest procedural-confidence
  signal available to a software project: *the history is growing*;
- and a **direction scan**, which chooses the next branch and thereby guarantees there is one.

The loop is self-sustaining. It has no natural exit. Each cycle is individually defensible and the sequence is
undefeated — which is exactly the property v2.45 named as a warning: **a process that cannot come out wrong is
scaffolding.**

**Vulnerability: LIVE, and this document is inside it.**

## 4. Most Suspicious Procedural-Confidence Patterns

*Where the workflow may make the project feel more advanced than the claim locks justify. These are the patterns; not
one of them is a conclusion.*

```text
P1. THE TEMPLATE RHYTHM. Proposal -> plan -> review -> findings -> synthesis -> scan -> proposal. Applied to a branch
    with evidence and to a branch with none, it produces documents of identical shape. Shape is therefore not
    informative about content -- and we have been reading shape.

P2. ASCENDING VERSION NUMBERS AS A PROGRESS SIGNAL. The number goes up whatever happens. It went up through every
    negative result, every pause, and every closure. It will go up when this document is committed.

P3. FOUR TERMINATIONS READ AS FOUR STAGES. Symbolic branch paused; null-first paused; anti-inevitability closed as
    non-authorizing; proxy / confound closed as non-authorizing. Each honestly labelled; the SEQUENCE still feels like
    progress. Nothing in the record corrects for this, and nothing can, because each closure is individually accurate.

P4. THE LOCK LIST AS AN INSTRUMENT PANEL. A tall, aligned column of False. Its formal density is the most
    scientific-LOOKING object in the entire record, and it is a list of things we have not shown.

P5. CODEX ACCEPTANCE AS EPISTEMIC WARRANT. ACCEPT AS-IS means the document obeyed its own constraints. It is
    conformance, not corroboration. A long unbroken run of acceptances reads as a long unbroken run of being right.

P6. THE DIRECTION SCAN AS A CONTINUATION ENGINE. Every direction-choosing document in this record has, in the end,
    produced a direction. The scan is nominally free to recommend stopping, and stopping has been named as legitimate
    every single time -- and it has not once been taken. A choice that is always available and never chosen is worth
    looking at directly.

P7. AND THE ONE THAT LANDS HERE. THIS SCAN IS AN INSTANCE OF THE TEMPLATE IT IS SCANNING. v2.52 planned it, this
    document performs it, v2.54 would review it, and a synthesis would close it -- the exact five-beat shape of the two
    branches that closed before it as producing no evidence. If P1 through P6 are real, the artifact-route branch is
    not immune to them by virtue of being ABOUT them. Writing a document about document-artifacts is not a defence
    against document-artifacts; on the current evidence it is the most sophisticated available FORM of one.
```

**And the honest weight of that:** P7 is not a rhetorical flourish. v2.52 pre-committed the project to taking it
seriously — it stated that `C` / `D` must remain better-supported than `A` **if the loop starts producing confidence
instead of falsification pressure**. This scan's own content is the strongest evidence yet assembled *in the record's own
terms* that it has. That is a statement about our process. It is not a statement about colour, or about vision, or about
anything in the world.
