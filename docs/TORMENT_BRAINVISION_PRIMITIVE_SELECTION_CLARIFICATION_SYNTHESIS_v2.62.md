## 3. What The Branch Accomplished — Non-Authorizing Only

**It clarified the bridge as conceptual only.** The chain was written down so it could be **argued with**, not inherited
— and it was written down with the warning attached: a clean row of arrows reads as a design and establishes nothing.

**It treated the arrows as assumptions, not working steps.** Four assumptions, not four stages of work. Three of them had
never been examined anywhere, and the quietest — that a summary written in *our* terms would be usable by a model in
*its* terms — remains entirely untouched.

**It made primitive selection visible as a missing question.** The programme had been asking whether a *chosen*
instrument measures well. It had never asked whether it is the right *kind* of instrument. That question was surfaced,
named, and left open.

**It caught the primitive-list drift.** The document that diagnosed the missing selection question also supplied a
vocabulary in place of an answer, and the review caught it.

**It admitted the re-import rather than claiming escape.** The successor's eight families **substantially re-imported**
the earlier vocabulary — a merge and a rename, not an independent arrival — and the document says so. They are
**unjustified candidate vocabulary**, not a taxonomy, baseline, sufficient set, or successor obligation. Two unjustified
vocabularies agreeing with each other is the same unjustified vocabulary counted twice, and that reading is forbidden.

**It pressured one high-risk assumption without selecting or validating it.** Pressure / salience was pressed and
deflated: *"demands attention"* has no content until you say **attention of whom, and for what** — salience is a
**relation between a stream and a purpose**, not a property of a stream. That is a **structural** observation, not a
proxy risk. Nothing was selected. Nothing was validated. Nothing was measured.

**It preserved the memory slide.** *"Attention-worthy"* becomes *"memory-worthy"* in one word, and nobody notices the
word change. **A salience primitive would be, functionally, a memory admission policy wearing a perception label.** The
hazard is named. It is not addressed, and naming it addresses nothing.

**It exposed lock-as-license as a live hazard.** A constraint written down begins to feel like a constraint kept — and
the material a constraint excludes is very often the material that would make the argument persuasive, which is *why* it
was excluded. The lock gets spent as cover.

**It preserved operator intuition as direction-choice evidence only.** It may guide *which* assumption is clarified or
pressed next. It validates nothing, invalidates nothing, moves no lock, and shortens no gate.

**It preserved all locks and HOLD.** Not one moved. Not one could have.

## 6. The Workflow Finding — Stated Once, Because It Outlives The Branch

The branch's most transferable product is not about geometry at all.

**Asked a selection question, this loop produces a vocabulary** — because a list is the only thing a document can
produce, and the question has no documentary answer. It did so, was caught, was rewritten specifically to avoid doing so,
and did so again with a claim of escape attached. It named artifact-generating structures, marked them, and printed them
anyway. It declared a boundary and then spent it, because the excluded material was the persuasive material.

**Every one of those was caught from outside the document that committed it. Not one was resisted in advance** — and in
the sharpest case, the hazard was named with precision *by the document that was committing it*. **The seeing and the
doing run independently.**

Which yields the only operational conclusion this branch has earned: **the circuit breaker is not comprehension. It is
external review** — a reader who does not share the document's momentum, checking the text rather than the intention.
That is not ceremony, and it must not be shortened on the grounds that the hazard is now understood. **Understanding it
is exactly what failed.**

**LOCK-AS-LICENSE REMAINS A LIVE HAZARD.** Named here. Not fixed here.
