## 5. Gate A dependencies

Gate D would depend on Gate A being genuinely satisfied, not merely framed:

- **The containment wall remains unbuilt.** Gate A's wall today holds by absence /
  unwired topology, not by a mechanism: no candidate-shaped output is produced, so
  nothing exists to contain. Gate D presupposes a *real* wall; an absence-wall is
  not one.
- **Layers 1–3 are requirements only.** The producer-shaped contract, the
  seam-selection comparison, and the candidate boundary / admission requirements
  are requirement-level design artifacts. No producer exists, no seam is selected,
  and no boundary or admission is built. Requirements are not a wall.
- **Layer 4 mechanics are not authorized.** The actual containment mechanism is
  Layer 4 runtime territory and is explicitly unauthorized. Until wall mechanics
  are separately authorized and built, **Gate D runtime cannot be opened** — a
  private cognition with no built wall to contain its outputs would have no
  containment.

## 6. Gate B / writer-authority dependencies

Gate D would depend on writer-authority boundaries holding, so that private
cognition can never become authority:

- **Private cognition must never create** canon, identity-tier material, seed
  material, long-half-life material, promotion, or any writer effect — directly or
  by side effect of being produced, staged, inspected, or retrieved.
- These obligations restate A-O1 (class-bound writer authority) and A-O2 (no silent
  canon / identity from cognition); Gate D may not weaken them.
- **The four parked writer non-conformances remain parked** (`gravity_correction`
  canon=True, `_maybe_emit_identity_anchor`, `/promote` force, `mood_drift → canon`)
  and outside this map's authority; this map does not fix, normalize, or reclassify
  them.

## 7. Audit-lane dependencies

Gate D would depend on the audit lane staying observation-only:

- **Observation must remain non-control, non-reentrant, and packet-blind.** Audit
  may observe authority; it must not become authority, must not feed back into
  cognition or control, and its evidence packets must drive no branch.
- **Envelope Audit remains unopened** (recorded posture: DEFER pre-substrate). Gate
  D does not open it and does not depend on opening it.
- Restating doctrine: audit observes authority; audit must not become authority.

## 8. Existing cognition-shaping dependencies

Gate D would depend on the existing context-shaping lanes staying shaping-only:

- **MemoryPlan / read-side shaping may guide context** — default-off bounded
  lane-weight guidance and read-side projection-safety are guidance surfaces that
  shape what context is offered.
- **They must not become private cognition, authority, persistence, or output
  control.** Guidance that shapes context is not the same as an interior that
  thinks; Gate D may not let shaping drift into cognition, into authority, into
  durable state, or into control of output.
