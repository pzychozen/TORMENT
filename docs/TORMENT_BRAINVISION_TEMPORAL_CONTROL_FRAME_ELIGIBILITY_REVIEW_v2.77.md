## 3. Scientific obligation versus control construction

The central distinction of this review, applied in every section below:

```text
scientific explanatory obligation   !=   specific control construction
```

```text
spectrum-accounting obligation      !=  spectrum-matched surrogate construction
roughness-accounting obligation     !=  roughness-matched construction
continuity-accounting obligation    !=  continuity-matched construction
phase-confound obligation           !=  phase-altered fixture construction
positive-control obligation         !=  a fixture declared positive by construction label
```

An obligation names an explanation that must receive an honest opportunity to win, or a claim that must be guarded.
A construction is one particular way of discharging an obligation. **No particular matching or surrogate mechanism is
declared mandatory here merely because the corresponding explanatory alternative must receive an honest opportunity**
— an obligation can, in principle, be discharged by different routes, or turn out to be dischargeable by none, in
which case the honest outcome is UNINFORMATIVE or PAUSE rather than a manufactured construction.

## 4. Spectrum-accounting obligation

**Determination: a future plan must give amplitude-spectrum magnitude and closely related spectral explanations an
honest opportunity to explain any apparent result.** This obligation is definitional: the target is stated as a
subtraction requirement whose first subtrahend is the amplitude spectrum. A plan without spectrum accounting is not
testing this target.

**The obligation is expressible without assuming that an exact spectrum-matched pair is feasible or scientifically
adequate.** The obligation is: "before any apparent organization is interpreted, spectrum-level alternatives must
have had a genuine, predeclared opportunity to explain it — and if no honest opportunity can be constructed, the
result is UNINFORMATIVE." That statement selects no surrogate algorithm, assumes no matching feasibility, and
survives even if exact matching proves impossible or itself answer-baking (a hazard the record already names: the
surrogate's construction may bake the result). The bounded v2.76 wording is preserved in force: under fixed
finite-series, normalization, and boundary conventions, Fourier magnitude / power-spectrum information constrains the
autocorrelation quantities used by an analysis; **practical autocorrelation estimates are not claimed to be
unconditionally fixed by a loosely defined amplitude spectrum.**

## 5. Roughness/smoothness-accounting obligation

**Determination: roughness and smoothness must receive explicit explanatory opportunities.** This is the record's
dominant confound family — SAG's apparent order fell to roughness/whitening; recurrence behavior was
roughness-sensitive — so a plan silent on roughness is not credible on this terrain.

**Exact roughness matching is not required.** The repository does not establish that exact matching is necessary or
honest for this lane, and the matching risks are recorded as live: matching can **conceal relevant structure** (what
is matched away can no longer speak); **manufacture similarity** (matched pairs can be similar only in the matched
coordinates); **destroy the target** (arrangement structure may covary with what matching removes); **create
impossible obligations** (exact matching on continuous properties may be unattainable, converting science into
tuning); and **produce false confidence** ("matched, therefore controlled" is a forbidden inference everywhere in
this record). The obligation stands; the construction is unresolved.

## 6. Continuity-accounting obligation

**Determination: local continuity must receive an honest explanatory opportunity distinct from roughness and
spectrum.** The record treats continuity as its own killer — the recurrence line's candidate largely tracked, or was
confounded with, continuity — and v2.76 established that continuity is accepted explanatory terrain to be beaten,
**not** asserted as mathematically identical to spectrum or roughness in all settings, so it is not universally
collapsible into either. Continuity matching is **not assumed feasible**; the obligation is the explanatory
opportunity, not a matched construction.

## 7. Phase-confound obligation

**Determination: a future plan must prevent phase sensitivity alone from being promoted into order-beyond-spectrum
evidence.** The conceptual guard is a claim rule, not a fixture: sensitivity to a phase-manipulating operation is
instrument behavior under an operation; it becomes evidence only inside a control frame that separately removed
roughness, continuity, and construction-identity explanations and gave spectrum-level alternatives their
opportunity. No phase-altered construction is designed here. Preserved:

```text
phase information may be relevant   !=   phase sensitivity validates temporal organization
```

## 8. Construction-identity obligation

declared before evaluation; and **the candidate measurement must not define its own controls** — a control chosen
because the candidate responds to it correctly is the candidate certifying itself. This obligation is required; its
discharge route is unresolved.

## 10. Positive-control honesty

**The question: can a positive-control obligation be stated without circularly declaring a construction to contain
the target?**

The circularity hazard is specific here. For a target defined by subtraction, the obvious candidate positive controls
are all forbidden shapes: a fixture expected to produce a high recurrence score (candidate-instrument circularity); a
construction containing the same motif the descriptor measures (descriptor circularity); a class labeled ordered by
its generator (label circularity); a phase construction selected because RPSR separates it (selection circularity).

**Determination: the obligation is conceptually poseable in a non-circular form**, namely:

```text
Before any target reading is interpreted, the harness must demonstrate, through a separately authorized and
predeclared certification obligation, that it can respond to a predeclared organization property independently
justified for certification purposes -- where the expected behavior is justified INDEPENDENTLY of (a) the
construction's label, and (b) the candidate measurement's own response to it -- and any failure of a required
certification returns UNINFORMATIVE with nothing banked.
```

This statement selects no construction and does not assume the scientific target exists as a real, measurable, or
detected phenomenon. It states only that any later certification would need a predeclared organization property
independently justified for certification purposes, separate from the construction label, generator vocabulary,
planted motif, expected descriptor response, and candidate measurement output. The load-bearing and **unresolved** part is the independence requirement:
**no concrete route to independent justification is currently identified in the record.** A derivation from
generation mechanics exceeds a bare label, but whether any such derivation can be made genuinely independent of the
construction's own vocabulary — and verified by something other than the candidate instrument — is exactly the
strongest blocker (Section 14) wearing its positive-control face. If every available route collapses into label
declaration or candidate self-certification, the honest later outcome is NO_HONEST_CONTROL_FRAME_POSEABLE or PAUSE,
and this review does not foreclose that. Preserved in force:

```text
failed required positive control
=> target result UNINFORMATIVE
=> no target score banked
```

Conclusion for this domain: **conceptually poseable; not concretely constructible on the present record; construction
unresolved; failure route explicit.** A positive answer was not forced: the poseability finding is about the
obligation sentence above, nothing more.

## 11. Failure and uninformative semantics

**Determination: the required meanings are conceptually distinguishable before any experiment is designed.**

```text
SUPPORTED_UNDER_TESTED_CONTROLS   apparent organization survives; all required controls valid; cheap explanations
                                  had genuine opportunities and lost. "Tested" is load-bearing.
PROXY_EXPLANATION                 a cheap alternative (roughness / spectrum / continuity / construction identity)
                                  explains the appearance.
ENTANGLED_OR_INSEPARABLE          candidate organization and cheap explanation cannot be separated by the tested
                                  frame; a first-class outcome, not an error.
ARTIFACT                          the appearance is fixture-made or harness-made.
NULL                              nothing separates anywhere; no contrast to interpret.
NEGATIVE_RESULT                   controls valid within the predeclared tested scope; the tested candidate
                                  measurement family / construction family / control frame received its fair
                                  opportunity; no beyond-spectrum organization was found there -- scientifically
                                  valid only as bounded evidence against that tested route or scope, not against
                                  order-beyond-spectrum generally.
CONSTRUCTION_FAILURE              a construction failed its own declared properties; says nothing about the target.
UNINFORMATIVE                     a required control failed, collapsed, or did not certify -- interpretation is
                                  withheld entirely.
```

NEGATIVE and UNINFORMATIVE are distinguished by **control validity**: NEGATIVE requires certified controls;
UNINFORMATIVE is what certification failure forces. Preserved semantics:

```text
failed required control                                        => UNINFORMATIVE

continuity                                                     => PROXY, ENTANGLED, or UNINFORMATIVE -- not support
absence of separation                                          => may be NULL, NEGATIVE, CONSTRUCTION_FAILURE, or
                                                                  UNINFORMATIVE, depending on control validity
```

No gate or threshold is operationalized.
