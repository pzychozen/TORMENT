## 2. Why Pressure / Salience Is Being Pressured First

Because pressure / salience is plausibly close to several live proxy, artifact, vocabulary, and workflow hazards, and
therefore an honest conceptual pass may return an uncomfortable answer.

```text
generic chroma                 -- a live proxy route, not ruled out here.
intensity / contrast           -- a live proxy route, not examined here.
roughness / spectral richness  -- a live proxy-risk shape, not reread here.
texture complexity             -- a neighboring proxy / artifact risk, not separated here.
attention-like language        -- "stands out", "demands attention" -- our words, doing work we have not paid for.
reporting salience             -- what our documents choose to mention is itself a salience filter, applied by us.
fixture emphasis               -- a constructed case may emphasize what it was constructed to emphasize.
operator / model focus         -- what the operator and the models find interesting shapes what gets written down.
```

**Note what the last four have in common:** they are not properties of streams at all. They are properties of **us** —
our language, our reporting, our fixtures, our attention. **Salience is the one family where the observer is inside the
concept**, and that is the deepest reason to distrust it (Section 5, and especially §5.9).

**A hard boundary, set here and binding:** this is **not** a proxy / confound rereading slice. That branch **closed** at
v2.50, and v2.50 forbade another. Pressure / salience is being examined as a **conceptual assumption about what to
read**, not as a re-examination of frozen proxy evidence. **No frozen result is reread, recomputed, requoted, or
reinterpreted here.** If this document starts to look like a proxy rereading, that is a violation, not a bonus.

## 3. Conceptual Meaning

In plain terms, and conceptually only — no metrics, descriptors, fields, scores, thresholds, or data structures:

```text
Something in the stream appears to DEMAND ATTENTION -- because it has relative contrast, chroma weight, texture
pressure, motion pressure, interruption, recurrence, or relation-pressure.
```

**Now read that sentence again and notice what is wrong with it.**

> **"Demands attention" — whose attention? For what purpose?**
>
> Persistence can be stated without an observer: *something stayed while other things changed.* Coupling can:
> *two things changed together.* **Salience cannot.** There is no fact of the matter about what "stands out" until you
> say **stands out to whom, and for what**. Salience is not a property of a stream. **It is a relation between a stream
> and a purpose.**

That is not a quibble. It is the whole problem, and Section 5.9 is where it lands.

## 4. Why It Might Matter

Stated only as a **possible bridge assumption**, and no more:

```text
IF a future system ever reads stream geometry, pressure / salience MIGHT help identify where event-like summaries should
look first.
```

That is the entire case. It is a claim about **where to look**, not about **what is there**.

**And it implies none of the following**, all of which remain false and forbidden: not detection; not validation; not
that any attention would be *correct* attention; not memory readiness; not runtime, screen, or integration readiness;
not vision. `candidate_structure_detected = False`. `vision_claim_allowed = False`.

**A further honest deflation:** "where to look first" is a claim about **efficiency**, not about **truth**. A system that
looks in the wrong place slowly and a system that looks in the wrong place quickly are both looking in the wrong place.
If the primitives are wrong (unresolved), salience makes the wrongness arrive sooner. **Ordering is not evidence.**

## 5. Proxy / Artifact Collapse Risks

Where pressure / salience could collapse — before any Brainvision-specific reading is entertained. These are **risks**,
not findings, and **naming a risk addresses nothing**.

```text
5.1  GENERIC CHROMA PROXY
     "Stands out" may just be "is more colourful". GENERIC CHROMA PROXY REMAINS LIVE, and it is not ruled out.

5.2  INTENSITY / CONTRAST PROXY
     "Stands out" may just be "is brighter or darker than its surroundings". This remains a live proxy-risk shape only,
     not examined here, and an unexamined route must not be upgraded into an explanation in either direction.

5.3  ROUGHNESS / SPECTRAL RICHNESS PROXY
     "Stands out" may just be "has more high-frequency content". This remains a live proxy-risk shape only; v2.59 does
     not reread, requote, or restate any frozen proxy / confound result.

5.4  MOTION / DISCONTINUITY PROXY
     "Stands out" may just be "moved, or broke". Motion is the cheapest salience there is, and it is not
     Brainvision-specific in any way.

5.5  TEXTURE COMPLEXITY
     "Stands out" may just be "is busier". A neighbouring proxy / artifact risk to 5.3, and not separated from it here.

5.6  FIXTURE CONSTRUCTION ARTIFACT
     A constructed case may emphasize what it was built to emphasize. A salience reading of a synthetic fixture may
     report the fixture's construction back to us. This is an artifact-risk shape only, not a rereading or requoting of
     any frozen record line.

5.7  VOCABULARY / SCHEMA ARTIFACT
     "Pressure", "salience", "demands attention" are ATTENTION-LIKE LANGUAGE. They import an observer, a purpose, and a
     value judgment in three words, and then feel like they described a stream. This is the symbolic-role artifact with
     an unusually good disguise.

5.8  OPERATOR / MODEL WORKFLOW ARTIFACT
     What the operator finds interesting, and what the models choose to write down, is ALREADY a salience filter. A
     salience primitive would formalize the filter that produced the record -- and would then be tested against that
     record. The circularity is not subtle, and it is ours.

5.9  UNRESOLVED ENTANGLEMENT -- AND THE STRUCTURAL OBJECTION, WHICH IS WORSE THAN ALL OF THE ABOVE
     Salience is a RELATION BETWEEN A STREAM AND A PURPOSE (Section 3). The purpose here would be "usable context for a
     system" -- which is THE BRIDGE. And the bridge, per v2.55/v2.56, REMAINS CONCEPTUAL and unvalidated.

     So: salience cannot be defined without the purpose; the purpose is the bridge; the bridge cannot be assessed
     without knowing what crosses it. THIS IS v2.57's CIRCULARITY -- and pressure / salience is the family that sits
     MOST DEEPLY INSIDE IT, because it is the only one DEFINED BY the downstream purpose rather than merely serving it.

     PERSISTENCE, COUPLING, AND RECURRENCE CAN BE STATED WITHOUT SAYING WHAT THEY ARE FOR. SALIENCE CANNOT.
     That is a structural vulnerability, not a proxy risk, and no amount of care about chroma or contrast touches it.
```

## 6. Memory-Bridge Risk

**This is where pressure / salience is most dangerous, and the danger is not subtle.**

> **"Attention-worthy" becomes "memory-worthy" in one word, and nobody notices the word change.**

v2.55 named the laundering hazard: a non-authoritative summary, stored and read back, tends to become a fact. Salience
makes it worse, and structurally so:

> **A salience primitive is functionally a memory admission policy wearing a perception label.**
>
> "What stands out" and "what is worth keeping" are the same sentence in different clothes. Any system that reads
> salience and writes summaries has, in effect, already decided what enters memory — and it will have decided it using
> an **unjustified term** (v2.57/v2.58), inside an **unvalidated bridge** (v2.55/v2.56), against **live proxies**
> (v2.50). Three unresolved things stacked, and the output looks like a memory that knows what matters.

**MEMORY INTEGRATION IS NOT AUTHORIZED. NO MEMORY BRIDGE FOLLOWS FROM THIS DOCUMENT.**
`memory_readiness_claim_allowed = False`. `integration_readiness_claim_allowed = False`. Nothing here creates a future
gate, and no later document may inherit warrant from this one, because it has none to give.
