## 2. Proxy / Confound Vulnerability Map (qualitative prose; no scores, no ranks, no criteria)

The lenses below are **suspicion lenses**, not categories that classify evidence. Nothing is sorted into them, and a
branch may sit under several at once.

**L1 — Generic chroma proxy.** The BY/chroma line is the most exposed place in the record, and it is exposed by its own
documents. Every closure audit in that line reached HOLD. The standing presumption — that an apparent residual *is* a
generic chroma proxy effect until a reporting-only distinction shows otherwise — was **stated, carried forward, and
never displaced**. `generic_chroma_proxy_ruled_out` and `by_residual_isolated` are both False, and nothing in the
frozen record moves them. A hostile reader can say of the entire BY line: *what you have is chroma organisation, and
you never showed it was BY.* The record contains no sentence that answers them.

**L2 — Intensity / contrast proxy.** This is the lens the record is *quietest* about, which is itself the finding. The
frozen material discusses chroma, spectrum, roughness, direction and movement at length; plain intensity, luminance,
brightness and distribution effects appear far less as *candidate explanations*. Absence of discussion is not absence of
confound. **The least glamorous proxy is the least examined one**, and its silence in the record should be read as
exposure, not as clearance.

**L3 — Roughness / spectral-richness proxy.** This is not hypothetical: the record **already documents it as an
unresolved confound**. The temporal-order line concluded that its apparent effect tracked *roughness*, not order. The
recurrence work then separated ordered from shuffled material but **failed its own roughness-invariance prerequisite**,
recording an inverse relation between the recurrence quantity and roughness. The colour-structure line likewise
decorrelated magnitude proxies while leaving a directional / per-channel spectral axis entangled. In each case the
record's own summary says: the confound was not dissolved. **Under this lens, several apparent findings in the record
read as roughness / spectral effects wearing other names.**

**L4 — Motion / temporal-discontinuity proxy.** `temporal_claim_allowed = False` exists *because of this lens*. The
order line's honest outcome was that its effect was **not temporal-order-specific**, and the follow-up returned a
**negative result on its own prerequisite**. The movement-matched colour work then found that matched pairs sharing
identical movement descriptors nonetheless separated — which the record reads, correctly, as evidence that the
separation tracked *construction*, not movement. Read together, the motion / discontinuity lens does not merely
threaten the temporal line: **the record already contains substantial concession language under this lens.**

**L5 — Fixture / artifact proxy.** A broadly documented vulnerability in the record. The movement-matched work stated
plainly that its separation tracked how the cases were built rather than what they were supposed to show; the
pooled-gate audit recorded that failing statistics behaved differently on the structural-null and control subsets than
on the clean matched subset — language that is **vulnerable to a scaffolding-driven rereading** of the aggregate
verdict. And one boundary-adjacent statistic is recorded as **platform-sensitive**: reported differently across
operating systems at the same nominal boundary. **This makes the result vulnerable to a platform / artifact rereading;
this scan does not conclude that explanation.** `artifact_ruled_out = False`, and a rereading cannot move it.

**L6 — Vocabulary / schema proxy — the reflexive lens, and the one this scan asks first.** Since the BY line stalled,
the project has produced: six conceptual roles, a role-reporting artifact, a reporting schema, an entanglement outcome
vocabulary, six adversarial family roles, a second role artifact, and a seven-document boundary programme. Every one of
those was careful, guarded, tested, and green. **Not one of them examined anything.** An unresolved state that has been
given named roles, a canonical schema, a checker, a boundary, and a closure *looks* far more structured than an
unresolved state that has not — and it is not. Under this lens, **a large fraction of the apparent organisation in the
record is organisation of our own vocabulary, not of any phenomenon.** The programme's own closure documents say this
in their own words: no evidence was produced; nothing could have been wrong.

**L7 — Entanglement proxy.** Every line that ran far enough to reach a verdict reached **HOLD**, and several reached it
*at entanglement*: BY versus generic-chroma pressure could not be told apart; the directional / spectral axis stayed
entangled after magnitude decorrelation; the pooled gate stayed unresolved. `entanglement_resolved = False`. The record
does not contain an isolated residual that is *waiting* to be extracted; **it contains repeated arrivals at "these
cannot be told apart", followed by vocabulary built on top of that.** Under this lens, apparent structure in the record
is the *shape of the mixing*, not a thing inside it.

## 3. Most Suspicious Branch-Language Patterns

These are wording patterns in the frozen record that can create the *appearance* of structure without reducing any
confound. They are described so they can be recognised, not scored.

```text
W1  "UNRESOLVED, BUT LOCALISED." Language that says a residual could not be separated, and then names WHERE it sits
    ("the directional axis", "the spectral axis"). Naming a location is not isolating an effect. The location language
    survives into later documents after the caveat has been dropped.

W2  "HOLD" READ AS "NOT YET". Every verdict in the record is HOLD, and HOLD means held for analysis. But a long chain
    of HOLDs reads, cumulatively, as a project one step away from a result. It is not. Nothing in the chain moved
    anything closer.

W3  NEGATIVE RESULTS DESCRIBED AS PARTIAL SUCCESSES. The order line's honest outcome was that the effect was NOT
    order-specific -- a real negative. It is recorded correctly, and it is also surrounded by language about what was
    "separated" and what "held", which softens it in the reading.

W4  CONFOUND NAMES BECOMING OBJECT NAMES. "Roughness confound", "directional axis", "spectral entanglement" begin as
    names for what could not be excluded and drift into names for things that exist and could be studied.

W5  SCAFFOLD COMPLETENESS READ AS COVERAGE. Six roles, six outcomes, six families -- completeness of a NAME SET reads
    as coverage of a PHENOMENON. The scaffolds themselves warn against this; the warning does not prevent the reading.

W6  PROTOCOL GREENNESS AS PROGRESS. Three artifacts are deterministic, guarded, and fully green. Green means the
    artifact said only what it was permitted to say. In a record read quickly, green looks like a result.

W7  "FIRST-CLASS ENDPOINT" AS AN ACHIEVEMENT. Making "entangled" and "collapse" sayable was honest work. It is also
    now a large part of what the record CONTAINS -- and a reader can mistake the vocabulary for the finding.
```
