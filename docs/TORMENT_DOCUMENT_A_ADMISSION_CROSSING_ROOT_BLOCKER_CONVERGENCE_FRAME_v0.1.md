## 3. Why the four arcs converge here

Each closed-for-now arc has independently reached the **same** downstream dependency: the Document A
**governed admission crossing** (the sole, operator-floored exit from containment) **and the candidate
carrier it requires**.

- **Gate A (containment):** the governed admission crossing is Gate A's **sole exit**; producer-independent
  wall work is saturated, and the remaining A-O3/A-D1/A-D2 obligations need admission mechanics + a
  candidate surface.
- **P4 (reader-trace / source-sameness):** O1/O2 have **no selected carrier or comparison mechanism**; the
  live joins are presence-only. A durable source-sameness reader-trace needs a **carrier** that survives
  `update_payload` canonical-last reappend and `eid` reuse.
- **Gate B (governed writing):** durable resolution of an authority-bearing write "likely depends on
  **carrier / schema / substrate + Document A admission**"; the writer side cannot finalize a governed
  crossing without the admission edge and its carrier.
- **Dream / Regime-B:** Document B **B-O9** permits the chamber to *stage* candidates but **not** to admit;
  the crossing itself belongs to Document A. Dream's staging→crossing therefore terminates at the same
  admission edge + carrier.

Convergence, stated once:

```text
Gate A sole-exit crossing  ┐
P4 O1/O2 source-sameness    │
Gate B durable governed     ├──►  Document A governed admission crossing  +  candidate carrier
   writer resolution        │        (operator-only floor already set;
Dream/Regime-B staging→cross ┘         carrier gated on Stage-B-framing / §K eligibility)
```

The **carrier** is the true hard dependency shared across P4 O1/O2 and Gate B durable resolution; the
**crossing** is the shared authority edge; both sit beneath the four arcs.
