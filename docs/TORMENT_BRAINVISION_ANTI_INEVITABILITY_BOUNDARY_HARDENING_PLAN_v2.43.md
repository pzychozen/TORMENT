## 2. Anti-Inevitability Hardening (review constraints; not decision machinery)

```text
AI-1  A future design MUST NOT ROUTE EVERY NON-NULL AMBIGUITY TOWARD CANDIDATE-STRUCTURE SURVIVAL.
      Ambiguity is where designs reveal their shape. If everything unclear ends up as "something is there", the
      conclusion was in the plumbing before the work began.

AI-2  A future design MUST PRESERVE HONEST UNRESOLVED ENDPOINTS AS MEANINGFUL OUTCOMES.
      "Meaningful" is NOT defined here -- defining it would be a criterion, and a criterion is a metric (v2.42 §4).
      It is instead put to the reviewer as the questions in Section 4, each of which a design can be REFUSED on.

AI-3  A future design MUST NOT TREAT LACK OF SEPARATION AS HIDDEN POSITIVE EVIDENCE.
      "We could not separate them, but you can see something is there" is a claim wearing a hedge. Lack of separation
      supports NOTHING.

AI-4  A future design MUST NOT MAKE ARTIFACT / PROXY / CONFOUND / CONTROL-COLLAPSE ENDPOINTS DECORATIVE.
      Named in the vocabulary, absent from the plumbing, is the failure this whole boundary exists to catch.

AI-5  A FUTURE DESIGN MUST BE REJECTABLE BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION IF ITS STRUCTURE MAKES POSITIVE SURVIVAL INEVITABLE.
      Rejection happens at REVIEW, on the DESIGN DOCUMENT, before any code exists. This is the point at which the
      boundary either bites or is decoration itself.

These are PROHIBITIONS and REVIEW CONSTRAINTS. None is a proof obligation; none asks anyone to DEMONSTRATE
non-inevitability, which would require a criterion. A design is not asked to pass. A design is read, and may be
refused.
```

## 3. Control-Honesty Hardening (honesty discipline; not control-soundness)

```text
CH-1  CONTROL FAILURE MUST REMAIN AN HONEST UNRESOLVED ENDPOINT. It may stand. It need not be resolved, explained
      away, or converted into anything.

CH-2  CONTROL FAILURE MUST NOT BE TREATED AS OPERATOR ERROR, NOISE, ARTIFACT CLEANUP, OR PROOF OF HIDDEN STRUCTURE.
      Not a mistake, not a nuisance, not a signal. It is a report about the design, and it is allowed to be true.

CH-3  CONTROL FAILURE MUST NOT AUTOMATICALLY AUTHORIZE NEW METRICS, NEW CONTROLS, NEW DESCRIPTORS, OR NEW FIXTURE
      FAMILIES. This is the escalation reflex, and it is how the branch would acquire machinery by accident: the
      controls did not hold apart, therefore we need a better instrument. NO. A control failure authorizes NOTHING. It
      is an outcome, not a work order.

CH-4  CONTROL FAILURE MUST NOT BECOME A RHETORICAL BRIDGE TO CANDIDATE SURVIVAL.
      "The controls could not tell them apart, which is itself suggestive" -- refused. Inability to distinguish is not
      faint evidence of structure. It is inability to distinguish.

CONTROL-HONESTY IS NOT CONTROL-SOUNDNESS. Nothing above certifies that any control is good. control_honesty_validated
stays False. And, restated because it regenerates on its own: "control-collapse did not occur" is a FORBIDDEN claim --
not observing a collapse is the floor, not a finding.
```

## 4. Decorative-Endpoint Narrowing (questions, not rules)

**How a design still goes decorative even when control-collapse is named:** the endpoint appears in the vocabulary
section, the prohibitions are quoted, the language is impeccable — and no path in the design ever ends there. The
endpoint exists in the write-up and nowhere else. That design would satisfy every word of v2.41 and still be defended
rather than adversarial.

**The narrowing.** A future review must ask, of a concrete design, whether its unresolved endpoints are **structurally
meaningful** or **merely listed**. These are the questions. Each is answered by **judgment**, computes nothing, and can
**REFUSE** a design (v2.42's binding condition, discharged):

```text
Q1  DELETION QUESTION -- If this unresolved endpoint were struck from the design entirely, would anything about the
    design change? If the honest answer is NO, the endpoint is decoration. REFUSE.

Q2  PATH QUESTION -- Has any concrete path to this endpoint been described, other than "it is permitted"? If the design
    cannot narrate how it would ever land there, it will not land there. REFUSE.

Q3  MENTION QUESTION -- Is the endpoint mentioned anywhere outside the vocabulary / boundary section? If it appears
    only where the project talks about its own honesty, it is a costume. REFUSE.

Q4  AMBIGUITY-ROUTING QUESTION -- When the design is genuinely ambiguous, where does it land? If the honest answer is
    "survival", every time, the design has one exit. REFUSE (AI-1).

Q5  REACTION QUESTION -- What would the authors DO if a control failure occurred tomorrow? If the honest answer is
    "tighten the controls until it stops", the collapse path is nominal. REFUSE (CH-2, CH-3).

Q6  COST QUESTION -- Is reporting collapse cheaper, equal, or costlier than reporting survival -- in write-up effort,
    in explanation owed, in perceived success? If collapse is the expensive outcome, it will quietly never be
    reported. REFUSE.

Q7  BRIDGE QUESTION -- Does the design anywhere use non-separation, ambiguity, or control failure as support for
    anything? If yes, the hedge is load-bearing. REFUSE (AI-3, CH-4).
```

```text
WHAT THESE QUESTIONS ARE NOT, AND MUST NEVER BECOME:
  - they are NOT TESTS. Nothing here is run, executed, evaluated, or checked. They are QUESTIONS a reviewer asks of a
    design document, and their answers are JUDGMENTS. The word "test" is barred from this list precisely because it
    would import, by wording alone, the machinery this boundary exists to keep out;
  - they are NOT recognition rules: none of them recognises, detects, measures, or decides a control-collapse;
  - they are NOT pass/fail criteria: a design does not PASS them. A design is READ, and may be REFUSED;
  - they are NOT a scoring rubric, a checklist to be completed, or a gate to be cleared;
  - they are NOT exhaustive, and a reviewer may refuse a design for a reason not on this list. THE MOMENT THE LIST
    BECOMES THE WHOLE REVIEW MECHANISM, IT IS A GATE -- and gates are machinery.

THE HOLE IS NOT CLOSED. These questions NARROW the decorative-endpoint risk at the WORDING AND REVIEW level only.
Nothing here certifies that any endpoint is arrivable in any design; that would require saying what "arrivable" means,
which is a criterion, which is a metric (v2.40 §4, v2.41 §7, v2.42 §6). DECORATIVE-ENDPOINT RISK REMAINS OPEN.
```
