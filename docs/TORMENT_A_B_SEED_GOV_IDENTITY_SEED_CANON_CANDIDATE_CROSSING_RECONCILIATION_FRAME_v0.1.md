## 2. Scope and purpose

The seam: **identity/seed/canon-affecting private-reflection candidates** — chamber reflection material
that seeks an *actual* seed, identity-tier, canon, promotion, or revision outcome. Both Document B (§8 /
Q-a) and Seed-Governance (§6) point at this seam and defer its unification to "later A/B reconciliation."
This frame writes that unification at requirement level and stops there.

Core frame (carried, exact): *Private reflection may stage candidate material. Staging is not admission,
projection, promotion, canonization, seed revision, or ordinary-memory write. Document A remains the
single governed admission edge. Seed-Governance adds stricter requirements for identity/seed/canon-
affecting outcomes; it does not create a rival crossing. Actual authored seed revision is not ordinary
admission and not an admission bypass; it is a separate governed seed-state revision boundary.*

## 8. Reconciled crossing contract (requirement level)

A single, coherent boundary statement for an identity/seed/canon-affecting private-reflection candidate:

1. **Chamber staging (Document B).** The candidate originates as chamber reflection and is *staged*.
   Staging is interior intent only — none of admission, projection, promotion, canonization,
   ordinary-memory write, seed rewrite, prompt visibility, model visibility, or cognition eligibility.
2. **Stricter class gate (Seed-Governance §6).** Because the candidate *seeks an actual seed /
   identity-tier / canon / promotion / revision outcome* (`identity-relevant ≠ identity-authoritative`),
   it carries the stricter Seed-Governance requirements. These requirements **ride on top of** Document
   A's admission edge; they are not a separate or rival crossing.
3. **Single admission edge (Document A §8).** The crossing — if it occurs at all — is **Document A's one
   governed admission crossing**, carrying the stricter Seed-Governance parameters. There is **no bypass
   and no rival crossing.** Its outcome is bounded by the released/low-authority ceiling; canon,
   identity-shaping weight, and unrestricted promotion rights are not conferred by ordinary admission.
4. **Separate seed-revision boundary (Seed-Governance §7).** Any *actual authored-seed change* is **not**
   ordinary admission and **not** an admission bypass; it is the separate governed seed-state revision
   boundary (operator-visible / operator-authorized / lineage-preserving / auditable / contestable /
   reversible; never automatic). A candidate seeking a seed change does not obtain it via the admission
   edge; the admission edge cannot stand in for the seed-revision boundary.
5. **No bypass invariant.** Staging never reaches an outcome except through the appropriate boundary;
   the admission edge never confers a seed/canon/identity *outcome* beyond the ceiling; and the
   seed-revision boundary is never substituted for, or substituted by, ordinary admission.
