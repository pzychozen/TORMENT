## 5. The Honesty Question, Answered In Writing (v2.49's condition)

v2.49 required this closure to answer, plainly: **is the negative reading being reached because it is true, or because
the programme is tired?** Twenty slices of vocabulary is exhausting, and *"it was all confounded anyway"* is a
comfortable exit that ends the work while feeling rigorous. The honest answer is **not uniform across the lenses**, and
pretending it were would be its own dishonesty.

```text
NOT TIREDNESS -- these would stand however rested the reader:
  - the roughness / spectral confound is recorded IN THE RECORD as unresolved. We are not supplying that suspicion;
    the record supplies it.
  - the temporal line's own summary says the effect was NOT order-specific, and the follow-up returned a negative
    result on its own prerequisite. That is the record's finding, not our fatigue.
  - the movement-matched work ATTRIBUTED ITS OWN SEPARATION TO CONSTRUCTION. We did not read that into it.
  - every line that reached a verdict reached HOLD. That is a fact about the document set, checkable by anyone.
  These are CONCESSIONS ALREADY IN THE RECORD. A well-rested reader with no history here would find them.

SUSCEPTIBLE TO TIREDNESS -- and therefore held more loosely:
  - L2 (intensity / contrast): the suspicion rests on the record's SILENCE. Silence is exposure, never explanation. A
    tired reader is exactly the reader who would upgrade "we never examined it" into "it explains everything". THAT
    UPGRADE IS FORBIDDEN, and this closure does not make it.
  - L6 (vocabulary / schema): the self-accusation is the most satisfying thing in the branch, and satisfaction is a
    warning sign. It is defensible AS A CLAIM ABOUT OUR DOCUMENTS -- they say of themselves that they produced no
    evidence -- but it must not widen into a verdict on the programme's VALUE, which is out of scope and which fatigue
    would happily supply.

CONCLUSION ON THE QUESTION: the LOAD-BEARING part of the negative reading rests on the record's own concessions and is
not fatigue-driven. The SUGGESTIVE part rests on silence and on self-criticism, and is exactly where fatigue would
push. This closure keeps the first and holds the second loosely -- and concludes NEITHER.
```
