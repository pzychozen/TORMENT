## 3. What The Branch Accomplished — Non-Authorizing Only

**It isolated one assumption to pressure.** After v2.58 refused to auto-ratify the broader primitive vocabulary and put
the choice to the operator, pressure / salience was selected as the single assumption to press — a **direction choice**,
never an adoption. Pressuring a member of an unjustified vocabulary justifies neither the member nor the vocabulary, and
the branch said so before it said anything else.

**It made the assumption intelligible — by deflating it.** The useful move was to notice what the ordinary phrasing
hides: *"demands attention"* has no content until you say **attention of whom, and for what**. Persistence can be stated
without an observer; coupling can; **salience cannot**. It is not a property of a stream. It is a **relation between a
stream and a purpose** — and the purpose here would be the bridge, which remains conceptual. That is a **structural**
observation, and it is deliberately not filed among the proxy risks: it is not a way salience might *collapse into
something else*, it is a fact about what salience *is*.

**It identified why the concept is dangerous near the bridge.** *"Attention-worthy"* becomes *"memory-worthy"* in one
word, and nobody notices the word change. Put at full strength: **a salience primitive is functionally a memory admission
policy wearing a perception label.** "What stands out" and "what is worth keeping" are the same sentence in different
clothes. The branch preserved that slide as a live hazard rather than resolving it — **naming a hazard addresses
nothing**, and it is named, not addressed.

**It kept the case *for* the concept honest.** The strongest thing that could be said is that salience might indicate
**where to look first** — an **efficiency** claim, not a truth claim. **Ordering is not evidence.** A faster route to the
wrong place is not progress.

**It preserved the allowed negative answer.** *Pressure / salience may be too proxy-heavy to carry bridge weight without
stronger falsification* remained available throughout, pre-accepted rather than discovered convenient — and **was not
taken**, because a negative that ends a branch comfortably is exactly the negative that must not be taken quietly.

**It exposed the lock-as-license hazard.** The branch's plan declared a boundary and then leaned on the very material the
boundary excluded, because that material made the argument land harder. The review that named the hazard then performed
it. See Section 5 — this, not salience, is the branch's most durable product.

**It preserved external review as a real circuit breaker.** Not ceremony. Twice in this branch the correction came from
outside the document that needed it, and both times the document had already declared the constraint it was breaking.

## 5. The Circuit-Breaker Lesson

```text
A PROHIBITION CANNOT BECOME PERMISSION TO USE THE PROHIBITED MATERIAL RHETORICALLY.
A REVIEW THAT NAMES A HAZARD MUST NOT PERFORM THE SAME HAZARD.
EXTERNAL REVIEW REMAINS NECESSARY BECAUSE THIS FAILURE MODE IS EASY TO REINTRODUCE.
```

This is the branch's most durable product, and it did not come from thinking about salience. It came from watching what
the documents did while they were thinking about salience.

**The shape of it, in plain terms.** A constraint gets written down. Writing it down feels like keeping it. The material
the constraint excludes is, very often, the material that would make the argument persuasive — that is *why* it was
excluded. So the document reaches for it, and the declaration on the page reads, to the writer, as the licence to do so:
*we said we would not, therefore we are safe, therefore this is fine.* **The lock is spent as cover.** It is not
carelessness and it is not bad faith. It is what happens when a constraint becomes **a thing you state rather than a
thing you obey** — and stating it is the cheaper of the two.

**The part that should trouble the operator most.** In this branch the hazard was named with precision by the very
document that was committing it — the review wrote *"a lock is obeyed, not announced"*, declared its own compliance, and
had to be corrected on exactly that point. **Understanding the failure mode did not prevent the failure mode.** The
seeing and the doing appear to run independently. Which means the circuit breaker is **not comprehension**, and cannot be
made of comprehension. It is **external review** — a reader who does not share the document's momentum, checking the text
rather than the intention. Every correction in this branch came from outside the document that needed it. **Not one was
resisted in advance.**

**And the honest cost of each correction:** the corrected text is thinner than the draft it replaced. The persuasive
force was on loan from material the document was not entitled to use, and when the loan was called in, the argument
shrank to its actual size. **That size is the truth of it.** A reader who found the first version compelling and the
corrected one thin has learned the right lesson, and it is not a lesson about salience.

**LOCK-AS-LICENSE REMAINS A LIVE HAZARD.** It is named here. It is not fixed here. Naming it is precisely what did not
work last time.
