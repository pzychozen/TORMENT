## 2. Branch Purpose

```text
THIS BRANCH ASKED: can future null-first synthetic design preserve HONEST UNRESOLVED ENDPOINTS -- above all
CONTROL-COLLAPSE, the endpoint at which the design's own controls fail to distinguish anything -- WITHOUT making
CANDIDATE-STRUCTURE SURVIVAL structurally inevitable?

It asked this because the BY/chroma arc had already shown the opposite failure: a design forced to sort cases into
"BY-dominant" or "generic proxy" will sort them WHATEVER IS TRUE, and the separation becomes a property of the design.
Null-first was chosen (v2.33) precisely because it could come out WRONG. Control-collapse is the mechanism by which it
could come out wrong ABOUT ITSELF (v2.38). This branch was an attempt to keep that mechanism alive.
```

## 3. What The Branch Accomplished (review / boundary work only)

```text
A1. IT DISTINGUISHED SAYABLE CONTROL-COLLAPSE FROM REACHABLE CONTROL-COLLAPSE (v2.39). v2.37 had made collapse SAYABLE
    -- a noun in a frozen artifact, costing nothing to say because nothing was real. Whether a concrete design leaves a
    path by which collapse actually occurs is a different property. Nothing has established it.

A2. IT IDENTIFIED THE DECORATIVE-ENDPOINT HOLE (v2.40): an endpoint named in the vocabulary, absent from the plumbing.
    Worse than no endpoint, because it makes a defended design look adversarial.

A3. IT SELECTED ANTI-INEVITABILITY + CONTROL-HONESTY as the safest NON-AUTHORIZING boundary direction (v2.40, v2.41):
    B forbids survival being the only exit; C forbids control failure being treated as a defect to fix. Neither works
    alone.

A4. IT HARDENED REVIEW / REFUSAL LANGUAGE (v2.43): AI-1..AI-5, CH-1..CH-4, and seven questions on which a concrete
    design may be REFUSED -- the first refusal-capable instruments in the branch.

A5. IT KEPT Q1-Q7 AS QUESTIONS ONLY -- not tests, not criteria, not gates, not metrics, not machinery. This required a
    correction: they shipped labelled "TEST" inside the anti-machinery document itself, and were refused and renamed
    (v2.43 MODIFY; v2.44 §4).

A6. IT REJECTED THE DIRECT ARTIFACT / IMPLEMENTATION TRANSITION (v2.42, reaffirmed v2.43, v2.44). An artifact cannot
    express B and C -- they are properties of a design and of its authors' reactions -- and a green checker over them
    would make a review-enforced boundary look mechanically guaranteed.

A7. IT KEPT HOLD / REDIRECT LIVE throughout, with pre-stated triggers, and never once treated continuation as the
    default.
```

## 6. Preserved Open Risk

```text
DECORATIVE-ENDPOINT RISK REMAINS OPEN.

  What was done: future proposals are now REJECTABLE -- by a reviewer, using judgment -- when they route ambiguity
  toward candidate survival (AI-1, Q4), or turn unresolved endpoints into decoration (AI-4, Q1, Q2, Q3), or make
  collapse the expensive outcome nobody writes up (Q6), or use non-separation as support for something (AI-3, CH-4,
  Q7), or would answer a control failure by tightening the controls until it stops (CH-2, CH-3, Q5).

  What was NOT done: nothing certifies that any endpoint is ARRIVABLE in any design. Certifying that would require
  saying what "arrivable" means -- a criterion, a decision rule, a metric -- which is exactly what the branch refused.
  "meaningful" remains, and must remain, UNDEFINED (v2.44 §5).

  So the guarantee lives in REVIEW AND JUDGMENT, not in machinery, and it is exactly as strong as the reviewer who
  applies it. That is the price of refusing machinery. It was paid knowingly.
```
