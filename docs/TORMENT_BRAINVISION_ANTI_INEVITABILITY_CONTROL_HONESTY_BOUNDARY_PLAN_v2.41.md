## 3. Central Question

```text
"Can future null-first synthetic design prevent candidate-structure survival from becoming structurally inevitable
 while preserving honest control failure as a valid unresolved endpoint?"
```

## 4. Principle B — Anti-Inevitability

```text
FUTURE DESIGNS MUST NOT BE STRUCTURED SO THAT CANDIDATE-STRUCTURE SURVIVAL IS THE ONLY MEANINGFUL NON-NULL ENDPOINT.

  - There must be somewhere else a design can honestly land. If every path that is not "nothing" leads to "something
    is there", the design has assumed its conclusion in its shape, and any survival it reports is a fact about the
    design rather than about the world.
  - B IS A PROHIBITION, NEVER A PROOF OBLIGATION. It says what a design may NOT be. It does NOT ask anyone to
    demonstrate non-inevitability -- because a demonstration would need a criterion, and a criterion is a metric.
  - B constrains SHAPE, not OUTCOME. It forbids a corridor with one exit. It says nothing about which exit anything
    would take, and it must never be read as predicting or preferring any of them.
```

## 5. Principle C — Control-Honesty

```text
FUTURE DESIGNS MUST PRESERVE THE POSSIBILITY THAT CONTROLS FAIL TO DISTINGUISH ADVERSARIAL FAMILIES -- and must not
treat that as implementation failure, operator error, noise, or hidden positive evidence.

  - The adversary must be allowed to lose its own case (v2.34 S5). A design that cannot discover that its own controls
    are meaningless is not adversarial. It is DEFENDED.
  - C FORBIDS A REACTION, NOT A COMPUTATION. It constrains what may be done WHEN a control failure shows up: it may
    not be filed as a defect and engineered away. Adjusting the adversary until it holds apart is v2.34 S2 violated --
    and it is the way this boundary would most plausibly die: quietly, in good faith, in a maintenance commit.
  - CONTROL-HONESTY IS NOT CONTROL-SOUNDNESS. C protects the possibility of failure. It certifies nothing about the
    controls, and control_honesty_validated stays False.
```

## 6. Principle D — No Recognition Rule (in force; restated, not weakened)

```text
v2.41 DOES NOT DEFINE how to RECOGNIZE, DETECT, MEASURE, SCORE, VALIDATE, or DECIDE control-collapse. No rule, no
criterion, no threshold, no comparison, no procedure. None is defined here and none is authorized.

This is not an oversight to be corrected later by whoever gets impatient. It is the constraint the entire line of work
exists to hold: a recognition rule IS a metric, and a metric is the thing whose absence makes this branch honest.
```

## 7. Principle E — The Decorative-Endpoint Warning (explicit)

```text
MERELY LISTING CONTROL-COLLAPSE AS AN ALLOWED PHRASE IS INSUFFICIENT.

A later design can satisfy every word of this boundary and still make control-collapse DECORATIVE: the phrase is
permitted, the endpoint is named, the vocabulary is impeccable -- and every path in the design still routes toward
candidate survival. The endpoint exists in the write-up and nowhere else.

An endpoint that is named but never arrivable is WORSE THAN NO ENDPOINT AT ALL: it makes a defended design LOOK
adversarial, and it launders the appearance of rigour. v2.37 made control-collapse SAYABLE. Nothing since -- including
this plan -- has made it REACHABLE.

B narrows this (a corridor may not have one exit). C narrows it further (a collapse that does arrive may not be
"fixed" away). NEITHER CLOSES IT. The residual hole stands exactly where v2.40 §6 left it, and this plan declines to
pretend otherwise.
```

## 8. Named Conceptual Hazards

```text
- CANDIDATE SURVIVAL AS DEFAULT ENDPOINT -- survival is where the design lands unless something stops it. Then nothing
  stops it, and the result was written before the work began.
- NULL / ARTIFACT / PROXY / CONFOUND ROLES AS DECORATIVE ONLY -- named in the write-up; nothing in the design turns on
  them; deleting them would change no outcome.
- UNRESOLVED OUTCOMES TREATED AS REPAIR TARGETS -- "unresolved" read as a to-do, and the design tightened until it
  stops occurring. This is how honest endpoints die.
- CONTROLS ASSUMED VALID BY CONSTRUCTION -- their distinctness is a premise, not something the design could discover
  to be false. Then control-collapse is unreachable by definition, and B and C are decoration.
- CONTROL FAILURE TREATED AS IMPLEMENTATION DEFECT -- the single most likely death of this boundary. It will not
  arrive as a claim. It will arrive as a bug report.
- ABSENCE OF COLLAPSE TREATED AS EVIDENCE -- "no collapse occurred, so the controls are sound". No. It is the FLOOR.
  Not observing a collapse is not evidence of anything, and "control-collapse did not occur" is a FORBIDDEN claim.
- POSITIVE STRUCTURE PROTECTED BY FRAMING -- the framing arranged so that a positive result cannot be dislodged: every
  adversary named, none able to bite. Rigour as costume.
```

## 9. Where This Boundary's Guarantee Actually Lives (the uncomfortable part)

v2.40 required this plan to confront whether B + C can be stated **without a criterion**. It can — but the cost must be
stated plainly, because it is easy to miss:

```text
B and C ARE CHECKABLE BY REVIEW, NOT BY MACHINERY.

  A reviewer reading a future design can ask: "is there any exit from this corridor other than survival?" (B), and "is
  a control failure permitted to stand, or is it a bug?" (C). Both questions are answerable by a person reading a
  design. NEITHER requires the design to COMPUTE anything -- which is precisely why B + C can be stated without a
  metric, and why they stay non-authorizing.

  BUT: this relocates the guarantee FROM MACHINERY TO JUDGMENT. There is no checker here, and there cannot be one --
  a checker would need a recognition rule, and a recognition rule is a metric (Section 6). The boundary is therefore
  exactly as strong as the review that enforces it, and no stronger.

  AND: the allowed phrase "control-collapse remains reachable but UNRECOGNIZED" is not a paradox -- it is the honest
  statement of this position. If a collapse ever occurred, no rule would announce it. It would be REPORTED, as a
  stance taken in a write-up, by someone willing to say that their controls did not hold apart. The honesty of that
  report depends on the honesty of the reporter, not on any apparatus.

WHETHER A REVIEW-ENFORCED, JUDGMENT-DEPENDENT BOUNDARY IS STRONG ENOUGH TO SUPPORT ANY FUTURE ARTIFACT IS EXACTLY WHAT
v2.42 MUST DECIDE. This plan does not assume that it is. If the answer is no, the correct move is HOLD / redirect
(v2.40 Option E) -- and that remains a legitimate outcome, not a failure.
```

## 10. Allowed And Forbidden Language

**Allowed** — and nothing stronger:

```text
candidate survival must not be structurally inevitable
control failure remains an honest unresolved endpoint
future designs may fail to distinguish adversarial families
control-collapse remains reachable but unrecognized
no recognition rule is defined
no validation follows
```

**Forbidden** — in any wording, as claim SHAPES rather than exact strings:

```text
control-collapse detected       control-collapse ruled out      controls passed
candidate survived              structure detected              null rejected
artifact ruled out              proxy ruled out                 confound controlled
descriptor validated            geometry validated              metric validated
screen ready                    runtime ready                   memory ready
vision achieved                 Brainvision sees
```

Paraphrases are the same forbidden move: "the controls held up", "nothing collapsed", "the adversary is in place",
"we ruled that out", "something real is there". And, carried forward and restated: **"control-collapse did not occur"
is itself a forbidden claim.**

## 13. Forbidden Drift Register

```text
- B becoming a PROOF OBLIGATION ("demonstrate that survival is not inevitable") -- which needs a criterion, and a
  criterion is a metric. B is a PROHIBITION.
- C becoming a CERTIFICATION that the controls are sound. C protects the possibility of failure; it certifies nothing.
- the boundary being read as a REACHABILITY CLAIM. control_collapse_reachability_validated, anti_inevitability_validated
  and control_honesty_validated all stay False.
- a recognition rule appearing "just to make the boundary checkable". That trade is the whole thing being traded away.
- the DECORATIVE-ENDPOINT HOLE being declared closed, or dropped silently from later documents.
- the JUDGMENT-DEPENDENCE of the boundary (Section 9) being forgotten, so that a review-enforced norm is later cited as
  if it were a structural guarantee.
- control failure arriving as a BUG REPORT and being fixed away (v2.34 S2 violated) -- the most likely death of this
  boundary, and it will look like diligence.
- "no collapse occurred" becoming evidence. It is the floor.
- a PLAN becoming an ADOPTION; a boundary becoming a licence; v2.42 being treated as a formality on the way to an
  artifact.
```
