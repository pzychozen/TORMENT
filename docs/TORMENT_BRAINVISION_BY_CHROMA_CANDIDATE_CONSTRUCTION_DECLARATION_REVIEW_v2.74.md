## 3. Declaration-review success standard

A candidate is admitted only if **all** of the following hold at concept level:

```text
S1. Positive conceptual identity: the declaration states what relationship makes the concept recognizable, not
    merely what it is not. A concept made entirely of negative constraints has not been declared.
S2. Question-bearing relevance: a coherent, non-answer-baked connection to the unresolved BY/chroma terrain,
    statable without treating opponent-axis naming, BY-axis alignment, winding, cancellation, or expected behavior
    as scientific truth.
S3. Scientific neutrality: no constructional identity encodes BY-specific, generic chroma, proxy, positive,
    negative, target-bearing, non-target-bearing, winder, canceller, directional success, expected descriptor
    response, or expected Path B response.
S4. No explicit or hidden winding load-bearing.
S5. All six interpretations remain reachable under the declaration.
S6. A non-outcome-based conceptual outside appears poseable.
```

Admission means **only** that a coherent abstract declaration exists for future docs-only review. It does not mean:
candidate selected; fixture family selected; membership specified; exclusion specified; generation feasible; control
feasible; L2 feasible; gate feasible; testing ready; or scientifically promising.

## 4. The neutrality-versus-relevance problem

Every candidate must survive both sides of one tension. **Requirement A (neutrality):** no scientific interpretation
may be encoded as constructional identity. **Requirement B (relevance):** the concept must not become so generic that
it no longer bears the BY/chroma question — "arbitrary chromatic relationships" is not enough merely because it is
neutral.

The trap on each side is known from the record: relevance bought with axis semantics, winding identity, or expected
behavior is the v2.27/v2.32 manufacture failure ("cases built for BY-leaning and for generic-proxy put the separation
back in the setup"); neutrality bought with genericity produces scaffolding that applies pressure to nothing (the
v2.50 vocabulary-loop warning: motion is not pressure). A load-bearing distinction used throughout this review:

```text
relevance via what a concept makes EXPRESSIBLE for the question
!= instances of the concept being DECLARED to carry an interpretation
```

The first is legitimate connection; the second is answer-baking.

**Name assessment and rename.** The word "grammar" carries executable-rule-language and generator-adjacent
connotation — a drift risk toward relation-operator lists and parameterized recipes, all prohibited here. The word
"neutral" invites the forbidden readings ("axis effects controlled"). The concept is therefore renamed, provisionally
and non-bindingly:

```text
Candidate A (renamed): "axis-unanchored chromatic-relation declaration"
```

declared identity of a construction under this concept makes no reference to any particular opponent-plane basis
direction. It does **not** mean: axis effects have been controlled; the coordinate basis is scientifically
irrelevant; BY-axis meaning has been disproven; or axis-related proxy explanations are removed. An axis-unanchored

is not an executable rule language, not a generator specification, not a list of relation operators, not an equation
set, and not a parameterized recipe; none is provided.

## 6. Candidate B declaration attempt

Provisional name under review: **"local/distributed chromatic-relation contrast."**

**What "local" and "distributed" could mean without equations, windows, scales, neighborhoods, counts, or
implementation rules:** chromatic-relational structure carried at small organizational extent versus structure carried
across larger organizational extent of a construction. The concept must not assume: local = generic proxy;
distributed = BY-specific; distributed = non-artifact; local = artifact; that either organization has expected
descriptor behavior; or that either carries target truth.

**Relabeling audit — the decisive failure.** The contrast dimension of Candidate B is organizational extent. In the
frozen record, organizational extent is the home terrain of the established proxy-risk families: roughness/spectral
richness (short-range versus long-range temporal structure), spread and centroid surfaces (distributional extent in
the plane — the per-channel blocker axis, `per_channel_C` bank-composition candidate, unresolved), movement and
trajectory complexity, bank-composition geometry, and distributed residual geometry. At concept level Candidate B's
poles re-partition exactly this terrain under cleaner words. That is the shape v2.50 warned of and v2.70 §7 barred by
synonym: an old proxy risk relabeled.

**Relevance audit — the second failure, independent of the first.** Asked what connects local/distributed contrast to
the BY/chroma question *without* a forbidden mapping, the honest answer found here is: nothing statable. The
available relevance route runs through exactly the prohibited identifications — "local relations are what cheap
proxies read; distributed relations are where a BY residual would live" — which maps organization to interpretation
(`local -> proxy-accessible`, `distributed -> candidate-residual`), builds the v2.27 separation into the setup, and
pre-bins future results into two expected branches. Stripped of that mapping, Candidate B collapses to generic scale
/ spread / complexity talk with no coherent BY/chroma connection — failing Requirement B. Relevance for B is
purchasable only at the price of Requirement A.

**Endpoint pressure.** The two-pole structure invites two result bins (local-explained versus distributed-surviving),
which pre-shapes `generic chroma proxy` toward one pole and crowds `entangled / inseparable`, `artifact`, and `null`
out of the natural reading — an endpoint-neutrality failure at declaration level.

**Declaration attempt, per required elements, recorded for completeness:** the declared relationship (relational
structure at contrasted organizational extents) is statable; the relevance element **cannot be stated honestly**
(above); the difference from generic scale/spread/roughness/complexity **could not be established** — the audit found
identity, not difference; what remains unresolved is everything, including whether any extent-flavored concept can
ever be posed here honestly; immediate invalidators were reached in this review (proxy relabeling; mapping-dependent
relevance); later membership and outside boundaries were not reached, and are moot under rejection.
